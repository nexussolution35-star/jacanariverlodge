Full-Day Kruger Safari Package

Kruger Full-Day Safari with Irving Knight Safaris at Jacana River Lodge

Luxury accommodation, game drives in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve and an unforgettable full-day safari in the Kruger National Park with Irving Knight Safaris.

2 Game DrivesIn the Mjejane

Escape into the captivating wilderness of South Africa with our exclusive package designed for a group of 10 or 8 people. Experience the perfect blend of luxury accommodation, thrilling game drives in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve, and an unforgettable full-day safari adventure in the renowned Kruger National Park with Irving Knight Safaris.

Package Highlights

Luxurious accommodation: enjoy 4 nights of comfort and relaxation at Jacana River Lodge, a 5-star self-catering retreat nestled amid the natural beauty of the Mjejane Game Reserve.

Thrilling game drives: embark on 2 exhilarating game drives within the Mjejane Private Game Reserve, guided by expert rangers who will unlock the secrets of the African bush and give you the chance of remarkable wildlife sightings, including the iconic Big Five.

Full-day safari with Irving Knight Safaris: journey into the Kruger National Park with Irving Knight Safaris, renowned for their expertise and commitment to providing exceptional wildlife experiences.

Full-Day Game Drive with Irving Knight Safaris

Departure: set out early in the morning to maximise your time in the park. The full-day game drive departs from Jacana River Lodge and takes you deep into the heart of the Kruger National Park, ensuring ample opportunity for wildlife encounters.

Wildlife spectacles: witness the incredible biodiversity of the park as you come face to face with majestic elephants, elusive leopards, mighty lions and a myriad of other fascinating animals. Irving Knight Safaris’ knowledgeable guides will provide insightful commentary, enhancing your safari experience.

Experience the thrill of the wild, the serenity of nature and the luxury of Jacana River Lodge with the Kruger Full-Day Game Drive with Irving Knight Safaris package. Contact us today to enquire about availability and secure your spot.

Five-Star Self-Catering Accommodation in Mpumalanga

At Jacana River Lodge we take pride in providing a 5-star self-catering experience. The 5-bedroom lodge is perfect for families, with a fully equipped kitchen that lets you prepare delicious meals using fresh local produce.

Nearby Attractions

While staying at Jacana River Lodge you have easy access to the many wonders of Mpumalanga. Explore the world-famous Kruger National Park and its incredible diversity of wildlife, or take a scenic drive along the iconic Panorama Route, with its views of Blyde River Canyon, God’s Window and the Three Rondavels.

Optional Add-ons

Enhance your self-catering getaway with optional add-on experiences: a guided safari led by experienced rangers who will unveil the secrets of the African bush, or a rejuvenating treatment with the Mjejane Mobile Bush Spa. Our staff are here to help tailor your stay to your preferences.
