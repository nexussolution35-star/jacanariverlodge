Unparalleled Accommodation

In the heart of the Mjejane

Are you ready to immerse yourself in the beauty of the African wilderness? Jacana River Lodge presents an exclusive 5-night package that promises an unforgettable adventure in the heart of the Mjejane Private Game Reserve.

Indulge in 5-star luxury as you spend 5 nights in our breathtaking self-catering lodge. With five beautifully appointed bedrooms, each boasting en-suite facilities and top-notch amenities, your comfort is our priority. Wake up to the soothing sounds of nature, surrounded by the sights and scents of the wild.
