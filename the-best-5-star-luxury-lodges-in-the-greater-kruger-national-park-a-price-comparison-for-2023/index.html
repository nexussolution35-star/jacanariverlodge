The Best 5 Star Luxury Lodges in The Greater Kruger National Park – A price Comparison for 2023

Published 23 March 2023 · Mjejane Game Reserve Expert

 1. Jacana River Lodge

Situated:

Mjejane Nature Reserve 

Look and feel?:

A front runner for its amazing location and hands down affordability compared to other game lodges in Mpumalanga, this 5 Star self-catering luxury Kruger villa offers 5 bedrooms and is perched on the banks of the Crocodile River. 

Main attraction:

The Mjejane Game Reserve borders the Crocodile river and of the Private Game Reserves bordering Kruger, is exclusive in its access to a private bridge into the Kruger National Park. It’s 4000 hectares boasts wild beauty where Kruger animals roam between its ranges and that of the larger national park.

Game Drives in the Private Reserve.

Great game viewing from the Lodge deck.

What reviewers say:

“Absolute heaven on earth, staff very helpful, venue 5 star ,the best views, logistics all in place, a definite recommendation. Will see you guys again. A special thank you to our reservation marketing officer Mariska Claassen, - great service” - Kaz Maritz

Ideal for:

Self-catering families.

Prices:

Mjejane Private Nature Reserve (accommodation starting from R 6000 per night for 2 guests)

2. Royal Malewane

Situated:

Thornybush Private Game Reserve

Look and feel?:

A sandy hued villa in the middle of the bushveld with amazing luxury provided to visitors and spectacular views.

Main attraction:

Butler services.

Safari Spa.

What reviewers say:

“Truly a magical location. The accommodations themselves are top tier, yet now slightly older as the resort opened in 1999. I say that to make it clear this is not the location with all the new architectural digest designs or latest in resort design principles, yet it exceeded expectations in every way.

The staff here was unparalleled. Nowhere else have I ever felt so cared for. So pampered. The people here, from the front of house to the spa to the guides/trackers took a vested interest in your happiness and executed at every step along the way. Whatever you wanted was never far away, all you had to do was ask and you’d be greeted with a genuine smile and interest. You will leave here with a new lease on life.” – Danny Slaughter.

Ideal for:

Couples

Pricing:

Thornybush Private Game Reserve (accommodation from R 8000 per person per night)

3. Singita Sweni

Situated:

Kruger National Park

Look and feel?:

Seemingly striding next to the Sweni river on stilts, this wonder of a lodge can accommodate 12 guests and provides intriguing connection to nature through architecture with floor to ceiling windows allowing the outside to almost pour onto the nature inspired furnished interior. Its something to experience.

Main attraction:

Gourmet experiences.

Floor to glass ceiling suites.

What reviewers say:

“Much smaller than its beautiful big sister Lebombo, Sweni is more intimate and quieter. The decor is bold and modern whilst retaining a friendly demeanor. A little too dark for my taste. The kitchen is open and you can watch the chefs create. The food is healthy and delicious. I received a warm welcome.” – Regina Fugat.

Ideal for:

Gourmets

Couples

Pricing:

R43,000 average per person sharing.

4. Sabi Sabi Earth Lodge

Situated:

The Sabi Sands Game Reserve

Look and feel?:

Blending with its surroundings, Earth Lodge is completely hidden arriving guests it is set into the bush itself, something to experience.

Its blends seamlessly into its wild surroundings but with luxury in mind no guest with be roughing it here, at one of the most well known Kruger luxury lodges in the world.

Simplicity and ruggedness combined expertly with the comforts of luxury accommodation without losing any connection to nature.

Main attraction:

Its unique architecture.

Large suites.

Understated interior design

What reviewers say:

“If only Google had 10 stars I would award it without hesitation! THE most spectacular time spent at Earth Lodge. Every single experience, moment, detail, service from staff just top notch! The wildlife was superb and you HAVE to ask Jabu and Leslie to be your Rangers - I have not enjoyed time out in a game park as much as this. The best sense of humour, knowledge of the animals and plant life and all round company. Each and every member of staff just so happy and well trained in customer service.

My compliments to ALL at the Lodge who made our time there so memorable. Thank you!” – Justine Edwards.

Ideal for:

Couples

Pricing:

Sabi Sands Game Reserve (accommodation from R 17 000 per night for 2 guests).

5. Dulini Lodge

Situated:

The Sabi Sands Game Reserve

Look and feel?:

Beautiful rock-built lodge nestled in one of the most famous private game reserves in South Africa.

Main attraction:

Plunge pools(heated) for suites.

What reviewers say:

“I went to Dulini River Lodge with my husband for our honeymoon, and it truly exceeded all expectations. I knew it would be a beautiful lodge and a great safari, but the people are really the best part. We ran into travel difficulties (cancelled flight) and they were quick to modify our stay and made us feel so comfortable and welcomed.

Every detail of the lodge is stunning and the experience is so well thought out. The food is top-notch! I'm vegetarian and I could not believe what the chef was able to come up with - I want a cookbook! The room is beautiful and feels like a true escape. I've been on safari before, but never on a private reserve and it was incredible how close we could get to the animals. Also, being right by the river allowed for great sightings even just from our room! We spotted a rhino from our plunge pool every afternoon!

Special shout out to our tracker, Observe, who has the most amazing bush eye for spotting, and another big shout out to Positive who's the kindest person. We felt so lucky to have Patrick as our guide who is thoroughly knowledgeable and gave us the best experience.”  – Monisha Kothari.

Ideal for:

Honeymooners.

Pricing:

Sabi Sands Game Reserve (accommodation from R 17 000 per night for 2 guests).
