Frequently Asked Questions

The lodge

How many guests can stay at Jacana River Lodge?

The lodge sleeps up to 10 guests in five en-suite bedrooms with king-size beds. It is booked on an exclusive-use basis, so the whole lodge is yours and is never shared with other guests.

Is the lodge self-catering?

Yes. The kitchen is fully equipped with a double-door fridge/freezer, gas stove, conventional oven, microwave and a Nespresso machine. There are two covered braai areas and a boma, with cooking tools and firewood supplied.

What makes Jacana one of the best game lodges in Mpumalanga?

Jacana River Lodge sits on the banks of the Crocodile River. It is secluded, big enough for the whole family and more than capable of meeting your luxury expectations, even though it is self-catered.

It is affordable for a big group sharing the cost, so you can extend your time in the reserve. It has real personality, with exquisite river views and open-plan living areas. The Mjejane is home to the Big 5 and so much more, and even from the deck you are likely to see all sorts of animals pass by. And it is exclusive: you and your family are the only guests at the lodge for your whole stay.

Is the lodge suitable for children and guests with special needs?

Yes. The lodge is child-friendly, with a one-of-a-kind kiddies' playroom and a jungle gym. It is also wheelchair-friendly, with one bedroom dedicated to guests with special needs.

Is there Wi-Fi and air-conditioning?

The lodge is fully serviced with a cable network and Wi-Fi. Every bedroom has air-conditioning, a ceiling fan, a bar fridge, a coffee station and mosquito nets.

Is there a cleaning service?

Yes. Our full-time domestic worker is available on weekdays from 07:30 to 12:00 and from 12:30 to 14:45, with shorter hours on Saturdays. She does not work on Sundays or public holidays.

Her duties are cleaning rooms and verandas, making beds, washing and ironing towels, and dishwashing. They do not include packing away guests’ personal items, preparing food, babysitting, or washing and ironing personal clothing.

Can we smoke at the lodge?

There is a strict non-smoking policy inside Jacana River Lodge. If you smoke outside, please use the ashtrays, make sure cigarettes are properly extinguished and don’t leave cigarette butts in the bushveld.

Can we bring pets?

No. Pets are not allowed in terms of the rules and regulations of the Mjejane Game Reserve.

Rates & booking

What are the rates for 2026?

Rates are for the whole lodge per night:

  • Low season, Monday – Thursday: R11 950 per night for the lodge
  • Low season, Friday – Sunday: R14 550 per night for the lodge
  • Peak season (long weekends & school holidays), Monday – Sunday: R14 550 per night for the lodge

Rates are subject to change. See Rates & Packages for current specials.

How do deposits and payment work?

A 50% reservation deposit is required to confirm your booking, payable within 24 hours. The remaining 50% balance is strictly payable 60 days prior to arrival. A fully refundable breakage deposit of R5 000.00 also applies, refunded after check-out subject to damage or breakage.

Are there any reserve fees on top of the rate?

Yes. Mjejane Game Reserve charges a conservation fee of R350 per vehicle (seven-day cycle) and R60 per guest per night, payable on arrival at the main gate. No cash is accepted at the gate; card payments only.

What is the cancellation policy?
  • An amendment to a reservation involving the arrival and departure dates is treated as a cancellation.
  • If you cancel 60 or more days before arrival, the 50% reservation deposit is forfeited.
  • If you cancel less than 60 days before arrival, 100% of the total booking fee is forfeited.
  • If the lodge is re-sold, a 25% administration fee is charged on the total booking fee.

Activities

Can we book game drives?

Yes. Private game drives in the Mjejane Game Reserve cost R2 000 for a group of up to 10 people and run for about three hours, either from 07:00 or from 16:00. Drives must be booked three days in advance. Guided bush walks, Kruger day safaris and a mobile bush spa can also be arranged; see all activities.

Can we do a game drive in the Kruger National Park?

Yes. As a Jacana River Lodge guest you can book a full-day (±12 hrs) or half-day (±6 hrs) game drive in the Kruger Park directly with the lodge. The vehicle takes up to 10 guests, with one of the Mjejane Game Reserve’s rangers. Rates exclude SANParks entry fees; see full and half-day game drives in the Kruger.

Can children take part in activities on the reserve?

Yes, but everyone under the age of 16 who takes part in activities on the reserve must be accompanied and supervised at all times by an adult or their lawful guardian. Please also supervise children around the outside of the lodge, which is in a high-density wildlife zone.

Is the Mjejane good for birding?

Yes. Many of the over 500 bird species that call the Kruger home in the summer months can also be spotted in the Mjejane, and over 330 bird species have been recorded on the reserve itself. Night birding is good too, with owls, nightjars, night-herons and thick-knees. See our Mjejane birding guide.

The reserve

What wildlife can we see in the Mjejane?

The Mjejane Private Game Reserve is on the southern boundary of the Kruger National Park, so guests can spot just about every animal the area has to offer, including all of the Big 5. Because the reserve is so close to the Crocodile River, wildlife sightings are especially bountiful, and you may even see the Big 5 from the comfort of the lodge.

Why stay in the Mjejane instead of inside the Kruger?

With its more exclusive appeal, the Mjejane is home to some of the most luxurious and private self-catering game lodges in Mpumalanga. Guests looking for a quiet escape, away from the busier parts of the Kruger, wake up to the sounds of elephants near their windows and fall asleep to the roar of lions, the cackling of hyenas and the calls of owls.

Is the Mjejane part of the Kruger?

Not officially, but it shares the Kruger’s wildlife. The Mjejane is a privately owned 4,000-hectare reserve on the Kruger National Park’s southern boundary. The fence between them has been removed, so the Crocodile River is the natural border and the Big 5 roam freely between the two, as part of the Greater Kruger. To drive into the Kruger National Park itself, you leave the Mjejane and enter through a SANParks gate such as Malelane or Crocodile Bridge, where Kruger conservation fees apply.

How does the Mjejane Private Game Reserve compare to other private game reserves?

The Mjejane Private Game Reserve experience far outperforms the standard private game reserve, as all fences have been removed between the Mjejane Private Game Reserve and the Kruger National Park, which is the third-largest game reserve in the world. This allows the free flow of one of the most diverse and most numerous animal populations on earth, which can walk right up to where you and your family are enjoying yourselves in 5-star self-catering luxury at Jacana River Lodge.

How big is Mjejane Game Reserve?

4000 hectares.

To think that the pristine wildlife reserve that is the Mjejane Game Reserve was once farmland is inspiring as an environmental rehabilitation feat. It now welcomes an amazing array of wild animals onto its 4000 hectares, which are connected without any boundary fences to the Kruger National Park.

Can I self-drive in the reserve?

Only on the main access road. Visitors may drive their private vehicles only on the main access road that runs from the entrance gate through the Mjejane Game Reserve to Erf 3, where Jacana River Lodge is located, or to the main lodge and offices on Erf 1. Deviating from the main access road is a material contravention of the reserve rules and will incur a fine. The speed limit is 40km/h, and unless you are in a designated area you must stay inside your vehicle. To explore the reserve, book a game drive. See rules & safety.

Is Mjejane a malaria area?

It is best always to be prepared with your mosquito repellent when heading to the Kruger National Park in the hot summer months, as the park does fall into a malaria area, which includes the Mjejane Game Reserve. For many visitors this is a minor concern, as cases rarely occur if proper care is taken to use mosquito repellents. Alternatively, plan your stay at Jacana River Lodge in the Mjejane Game Reserve for the winter months, when the mosquitoes take a hiatus. Do note that some towns and cities in South Africa are in malaria areas, with few cases occurring even in the summer months thanks to responsible human behaviour and residents taking precautionary measures. The reserve rules advise all visitors to follow a medical doctor’s instructions.

When is the best time to visit?

Every season brings something different. Many consider winter the very best time to visit the Mjejane: the lack of dense bush makes wildlife sightings incredibly common, and the dry winter months (May to September) are generally the best time for wildlife viewing. Summer brings thunderstorms, green bush and new babies. See the best time to visit Kruger.

Getting here

Where is the lodge?

Jacana River Lodge is in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve near Hectorspruit, Mpumalanga, on the banks of the Crocodile River along the southern boundary of the Kruger National Park. It is about 4 hours from Johannesburg and 1 hour from Kruger Mpumalanga International Airport, between the Malelane and Crocodile Bridge gates. See our directions for the route.

Where is Mjejane Game Reserve?

The Mjejane Game Reserve is situated at Nr 1 Mjejane Bush Camp Road, Hectorspruit, 2021. See our directions page for how to find us.

Which province is Mjejane Game Reserve in?

It is in Mpumalanga, on the southern boundary of the Kruger National Park.

Jacana River Lodge is perfectly poised to offer one of the most unique family 5-star self-catering experiences, as it is perched on the banks of the Crocodile River in the Mjejane Game Reserve. The Mjejane Private Game Reserve is situated in the Mpumalanga province of South Africa and allows Big 5 interactions that few other private game reserves can boast, as the wildlife from the Kruger Park freely enter the Mjejane Game Reserve.

Where is Hectorspruit situated?

Mpumalanga, between Malelane and Komatipoort.

Hectorspruit has two names, the other being Emjejane, and it is located between Komatipoort and Kaapmuiden, very close to the southern tip of the Kruger National Park and the Crocodile River in the Mpumalanga province of South Africa. Malelane is also nearby.

Can we drive into the Kruger from the Mjejane?

Yes, through a SANParks gate: leave the Mjejane and enter the Kruger at Malelane or Crocodile Bridge gate, where Kruger conservation fees apply, or book a guided full- or half-day Kruger drive with us. The Mjejane’s own bridge into the Kruger is temporarily closed. When it is open, guests can cross in their own vehicle (high ground clearance required) or hire the lodge game viewer for a full or half day. Self-drivers must leave through the main Erf 3 gate and keep strictly to the demarcated route. See the Mjejane bridge entrance to the KNP.
