A Weekend Getaway in Mpumalanga

Experience the best of Mpumalanga with Jacana River Lodge – your gateway to self-catering 5-star bliss. Escape to the breathtaking landscapes of Mpumalanga and experience the freedom and convenience of self-catering accommodation.

You can book the lodge for a full week or just a weekend, and you’ll never share it with strangers. We believe that a self-catering getaway offers a truly immersive and personalised experience, allowing you to create cherished memories with your loved ones while enjoying the comforts of a luxury game lodge in Mpumalanga.

Highlighting Unique Features

Nestled within the pristine Mjejane Private Game Reserve, Jacana River Lodge stands as an oasis of tranquillity. Our self-catering lodge offers you the perfect blend of privacy, luxury and convenience. Immerse yourself in the natural beauty of Mpumalanga as you relax in your private living space, complete with modern furnishings and elegant decor.

  • Private game reserve
  • 5-star rating on TripAdvisor
  • Endless photo opportunities

What’s Included

When you book, the whole lodge is yours: five en-suite bedrooms with king-size beds for up to 10 guests, open-plan living areas, a fully equipped kitchen, two covered braai areas and a boma (firewood is supplied), the rim-flow infinity pool and deck over the river, Wi-Fi, and our full-time domestic worker on weekdays and Saturday mornings. See the rooms and suites and our self-catering guide.

Things to Do Nearby

Nearby Attractions

While staying at Jacana River Lodge, you’ll have easy access to the many wonders of Mpumalanga. Explore the world-famous Kruger National Park, where you can witness the incredible diversity of wildlife in its natural habitat. Take a scenic drive along the iconic Panorama Route, marvelling at the panoramic views of Blyde River Canyon, God’s Window and the Three Rondavels.

Optional Add-ons

Enhance your self-catering getaway with our optional add-on services and experiences. Embark on a guided safari tour, led by experienced rangers who will unveil the secrets of the African bush (see our activities). Indulge in a rejuvenating spa treatment, allowing your cares to melt away in a serene setting (the Mjejane Mobile Bush Spa is booked directly with the spa). Our dedicated staff is here to tailor your stay to your preferences, ensuring a truly unforgettable experience.
