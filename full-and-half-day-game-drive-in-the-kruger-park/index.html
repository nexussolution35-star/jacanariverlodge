Book Your Full or Half Day Game Drive in the Kruger Park

A full or half-day game drive in the Kruger Park is one of those adventures that you won’t soon forget, and is offered as one of the activities near our lodge which can be booked on request. In the company of an experienced game ranger, and seated in an open-air safari vehicle, you will be in the perfect position to spot the big-name wildlife while learning more about the animals and plants you stop to observe.

At Jacana River Lodge, our guests can look forward to exquisite accommodation overlooking some of the best parts of the ever-beautiful Mjejane Private Game Reserve, as well as an exclusive safari experience.

Since the Mjejane borders the famous Kruger National Park, guests who’d like an authentic Kruger Park experience can opt to book a safari to the Kruger or in the Mjejane Game Reserve when booking their stay with us. The Kruger National Park is one of those places that will stay with you forever. It is a legendary destination, and one that is worth the trip.

Deciding if a Half Day Safari in the Kruger National Park Is for You

The game drive experience is unlike any other. Travelling slowly through the park, following the dusty, history-laden tracks that criss-cross this ancient nature conservation effort, all the while keeping an eye out for wildlife, is a superb way to spend a morning or an afternoon.

If you are on the fence about whether a guided half-day safari in the Kruger Park is something you should partake in, perhaps this will help.

A half day is more than enough time. A full day in the park can feel a little overwhelming for some, and this type of full-day trip is also going to cut into your holiday if you are only staying in the area for a few short days. With a half-day trip, you can see the best of the park while still having enough time to do your own thing.

A half-day tour can present the best sights. Some think that the only way to see the best of the wildlife the Kruger has to offer is to spend more than a week in the park. That’s not exactly true, as a half-day tour can quite easily result in seeing the Big 5 and more.

A half-day tour is stress free. Instead of having to worry about planning your trip and organising your drive to make sure you get to all the best wildlife viewing places, your tour will be stress free, allowing you to just live in the moment and have fun with it.

Please note: the Mjejane bridge into the Kruger is temporarily closed. Ask the lodge about current Kruger access.

Booking Your Full or Half Day Game Drive in the Kruger Park

When you are staying at Jacana River Lodge, you can book your full or half-day tour in the Kruger as a separate additional activity when you book your stay with us. If you have any queries, you can always send us an email with your questions and we will help you as best we can.

What You Should Bring

There are a few items that everyone should bring along on their game drive to ensure an amazing experience:

  • Binoculars
  • Bird book
  • Hat
  • Camera
  • Mammal book
  • Sun block
  • Reptile book
