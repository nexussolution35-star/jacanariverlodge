Five en-suite bedrooms

Rooms & Suites

Five king en-suite bedrooms in an exclusive-use, five-star self-catering lodge on the Crocodile River.

Exclusive use

Five King Bedrooms, All Yours

Sleeps up to 10 guests

Five en-suite bedrooms with king-size beds sleep up to 10 guests, with air-conditioning, a ceiling fan, a bar fridge, a coffee station and mosquito nets in every room.

Jacana River Lodge is booked on an exclusive-use basis, so all five bedrooms are yours and you never share the lodge with other guests. The bedrooms sit under the lodge’s thatched roof, a short walk from the open-plan living areas, the deck and the infinity pool above the Crocodile River.

The bedrooms

King Beds & En-Suite Bathrooms

Luxury and comfort await you. Lie back on an impeccably dressed king-size bed and wake to the sounds of the bush. Each en-suite bathroom comprises a toilet, double vanity and shower and/or bath, while some rooms also have an outside shower.

Every bedroom has:

There is limited electrical supply capacity in the reserve, so please switch off the air-conditioning, lights and appliances when you are not using them.

Accessibility

Wheelchair-Friendly Room

The lodge is wheelchair-friendly, with one bedroom dedicated to guests with special needs. If you have any additional requirements, contact the lodge team before you book to discuss your specific needs: call or WhatsApp +27 (0)66 293 7100 or email info@jacanariverlodge.com.

Beyond the bedrooms

Living Areas

The lounge, dining area and kitchen are spacious, yet have a homely, cosy atmosphere, and the lodge is fully serviced with a cable network and Wi-Fi. Step out onto the veranda for alfresco dining, relax in the separate seating area with views over the river, or jump into the rim-flow swimming pool. The upper deck and bar are ideal for get-togethers and for watching the sun set over the bush.

For the children there is a kiddies’ playroom with TV, a drawing board, books and games, plus a pool table, a small gym and a jungle gym on the lawn inside the walled courtyard. See all the lodge facilities and our self-catering guide.
