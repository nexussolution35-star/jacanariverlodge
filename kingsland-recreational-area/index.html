Kingsland Recreational Area

The Kingsland Recreational Facility

One of Mjejane’s hidden gems: walking trails, cycling routes and a bush braai under the Tamboti trees.

WalkingTrails
13.7 kmMTB Trail (Red Route)
12 PeopleBush Braai
48 HoursBooking Notice

The Kingsland picnic facility is one of Mjejane’s hidden gems. It is situated on the tranquil buffalo drainage line with lots of birdlife, and is the perfect place to unwind and relax under the Tamboti trees.

Kingsland Recreational Area is a safe, fenced-in area within the Mjejane Game Reserve, with various fauna and flora indigenous to South Africa. On Kingsland you can explore the walking trails, dare to take on the cycling routes and enjoy a relaxing bush braai that accommodates 12 people.

Trails

  • 13.7 km MTB trail (red route)
  • 7.8 km MTB trail
  • 3.7 km MTB trail
  • Walking trails

Booking Procedure

1. Bookings are essential for bush braais and can be booked with Melissa Gloy via email (hoa@mjejane.com) 48 hours in advance.

2. Melissa Gloy from the Mjejane HOA will confirm availability of the facility.
