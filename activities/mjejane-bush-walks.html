Mjejane Bush Walks

Explore Mjejane’s wildlife on foot with 3.5-to-4.5-hour guided bush walks every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.

3.5–4.5 hrsOn Foot
Tue · Thu · SatWeekly Walks
SeasonedGuides
UntouchedWilderness

Welcome to an extraordinary expedition at the Mjejane Private Game Reserve, where you can embark on an authentic African walking safari through untouched wilderness. Our guided bush treks offer an unparalleled opportunity to immerse yourself in the beauty of nature on foot.

Embark on a captivating 3.5-to-4.5-hour hike that places you in the heart of the African bush. Our seasoned guides lead weekly walks every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.

While a traditional game drive is exhilarating, nothing compares to the serene intimacy of encountering wildlife on foot. Stroll through an area untouched by human interference, free from exhaust fumes, and connect with nature on a profound level.

Good to know: To secure your spot, please enquire with us and we will arrange your walk with the reserve’s reservations team.
