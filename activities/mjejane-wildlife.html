Wildlife of the Mjejane

Wildlife & Big 5 Sightings

A wide variety of wildlife, including the Big 5, roams freely between the Kruger National Park and the Mjejane Game Reserve.

A wide variety of wildlife, including the Big 5, roams freely between the KNP and the Mjejane Game Reserve. That is why Jacana River Lodge has its own private game-viewing vehicle to enjoy the roughly 4 000 hectares of the reserve.

Because the reserve borders the Crocodile River and the Greater Kruger, early mornings and late evenings deliver the most bountiful sightings — leopards and elephants along the banks, hippo pods grunting from the water, and predators never far behind the plains game.

From the lodge stoep or out on a drive, recent spottings have included lion, leopard, elephant, rhino and buffalo, along with a colourful array of birds and plains game.

Because the fences between the Kruger National Park and Mjejane are permeable, wildlife moves freely between the two — so the reserve enjoys the full cast of the Southern Kruger without the crowds. The Crocodile River frontage concentrates that wildlife along the banks, especially at first and last light.

From the lodge stoep you might watch elephants drink, hear hippos grunt through the night, or catch a leopard slipping through the riverine forest. Out on a drive, the same 4 000 hectares deliver lion, buffalo, rhino, plains game and a wealth of birds.

What to Expect

  • Big 5 free-roaming between Kruger and Mjejane
  • Roughly 4 000 hectares to explore
  • Your own private game-viewing vehicle
  • Riverfront sightings — elephant, hippo, leopard
  • Plains game and predators
  • Wonderful viewing from the lodge itself
