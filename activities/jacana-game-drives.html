Private Game Drives

Jacana River Lodge has its own private, exclusive game-viewing vehicle to enjoy roughly 4 000 hectares of prime Big 5 territory in the Mjejane Game Reserve.

Whether it is a morning spent out on the open plains of the Mjejane Game Reserve, or a sunset game drive along the banks of the Crocodile River, a guided drive is one of those experiences that everyone visiting this corner of the world should book.

The Mjejane Game Reserve is a one-of-a-kind destination. As part of the Kruger Concession area, its 4 000 hectares are renowned as some of the best for wildlife viewing — and a guided drive in our custom-made open game viewer is one of the best ways to take it all in.

Wildlife, including the Big 5, roams freely between the Kruger National Park and the Mjejane Game Reserve, so no two drives are ever the same. In the peace and quiet of the private reserve you enjoy exclusive sightings, far from the crowds.

Your drive is led by an experienced guide who reads the bush like a book — following fresh tracks, pointing out the smaller ‘little five’, and positioning the open vehicle for the best light and the safest, closest views. Because Mjejane is a private reserve, you rarely share a sighting with another vehicle.

Mornings are cool and full of activity as the night’s predators finish their work; afternoon drives drift into a golden-hour sundowner, where you can stop for a drink in the bush as the sun sinks over the Crocodile River. Blankets and ponchos are on board for the cooler starts.

What to Expect

  • Big 5 territory across roughly 4 000 private hectares
  • Custom-built open game viewer with raised, tiered seating
  • Sunrise and late-afternoon departures
  • Expert local guide and tracker
  • Sundowner stop on afternoon drives
  • Rarely share a sighting — exclusive, unhurried viewing
