Kingsland Recreational Area

One of Mjejane’s hidden gems — a tranquil picnic facility on the buffalo drainage line with abundant birdlife.

PicnicFacility
BuffaloDrainage Line
AbundantBirdlife
PeacefulEscape

The Kingsland picnic facility is one of Mjejane’s hidden gems. It is situated on the tranquil buffalo drainage line with lots of birdlife and is the perfect place to unwind and relax.

Pack a picnic, settle in beside the water and let the rhythm of the bush slow you down — a quiet, scenic counterpoint to a morning game drive.
