Mjejane Bush Spa

The Mjejane Mobile Bush Spa comes to you with pampering, restorative massages and luxury treatments — deeply rooted in African tradition.

A safari holiday is a sensory experience, with an abundance of exotic yet earthy sights, sounds, tastes and smells. It is all about relaxing, deep breathing and slowing down.

Although the landscape offers a huge bounty of memorable moments, few experiences beat the sheer indulgence of a tailor-made massage in the privacy of your suite. Enjoy a relaxed, unwinding treatment while the kids play in the courtyard, overlooking the Crocodile riverbank and the Greater Kruger.

The Mjejane Mobile Bush Spa is deeply rooted in African tradition and themes, using the finest locally sourced products to leave you completely restored.
