Birding on the Mjejane

A window into the sensational avian wildlife that ‘twitchers’ are constantly on the lookout for in the Mjejane Game Reserve.

DiverseHabitats
SummerMigrants
RareSpecials
RiverineForest

Welcome to the Mjejane Birding Guide — a guide to some of the sensational avian wildlife that ‘twitchers’ (also known as birders) are constantly on the lookout for in the reserve.

Mario Paul: “There are some birds that occur on Mjejane which are very difficult to find in the Kruger National Park. These include African Crake, White-backed Night Heron and Temminck’s Courser, to name a few. The summer months are the most exciting, as this is when all the summer migrants are back on the reserve.”

Gavin Sims: “I had fantastic and numerous sightings of certain species that I have very rarely seen in the KNP — Pink-throated Twinspot, Green Sandpiper, Dwarf Bittern and European Nightjar.” Although the reserve is small, its diverse habitats attract a wonderful variety of species.
