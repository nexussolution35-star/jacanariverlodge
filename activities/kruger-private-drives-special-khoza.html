Kruger Drives with Ranger Special Khoza

Experience an unforgettable private Kruger safari with Ranger Special Khoza — expert-guided game drives and intimate encounters with Africa’s iconic wildlife.

PrivateGuided Drives
Big 5Expert Tracking
KrugerNational Park
LocalKnowledge

Meet Special Khoza, your Mjejane game ranger. “I grew up in a village called Lilydale, very close to Sabi Sand and the Kruger National Park, near the Paul Kruger Gate, so I have been in the Kruger many times since I was young — learning about wildlife as part of our studies during primary school. After completing my matric I earned a bursary to study nature guiding.”

For Special, the best part of the job is “being out there working physically in nature, meeting new people, expanding my knowledge of nature and learning about different cultures.” That passion shows on every drive.

A private drive with Ranger Special Khoza means the pace, the route and the focus are all yours — an intimate, expert-guided way to discover the Big Five and the smaller wonders of the bush.

On a private drive the vehicle is yours alone, so the morning belongs to your family or group entirely — linger at a leopard sighting, double back for the perfect photograph, or simply switch the engine off and listen to the bush. Special tailors every outing to what you most want to see.

Years spent guiding in and around the Kruger mean Special knows where the animals move with the seasons, which waterholes reward an early start, and how to find the quiet, off-the-beaten-track corners that most visitors never see.

What to Expect

  • A fully private vehicle — just your group
  • Guided by qualified ranger Special Khoza
  • Flexible route, pace and focus
  • Deep local knowledge of the Greater Kruger
  • Big 5 and birding tailored on request
  • Ideal for photographers and families
