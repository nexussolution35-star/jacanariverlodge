Activities in the Mjejane Game Reserve

From private game drives and guided bush walks to a mobile bush spa and world-class birding — here is everything to do during your stay.

Private Game Drives

Jacana River Lodge has its own private, exclusive game-viewing vehicle to enjoy roughly 4 000 hectares of prime Big 5 territory in the Mjejane Game Reserve.

Learn more →

Kruger Drives with Ranger Special Khoza

Experience an unforgettable private Kruger safari with Ranger Special Khoza — expert-guided game drives and intimate encounters with Africa’s iconic wildlife.

Learn more →

Kruger Safaris with Irving Knight

Enjoy an unforgettable Kruger National Park safari with Irving Knight Safaris, guided by experienced rangers who will help you discover the Big Five.

Learn more →

Mjejane Bush Walks

Explore Mjejane’s wildlife on foot with 3.5-to-4.5-hour guided bush walks every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.

Learn more →

Mjejane Bush Spa

The Mjejane Mobile Bush Spa comes to you with pampering, restorative massages and luxury treatments — deeply rooted in African tradition.

Learn more →

Birding on the Mjejane

A window into the sensational avian wildlife that ‘twitchers’ are constantly on the lookout for in the Mjejane Game Reserve.

Learn more →

Kingsland Recreational Area

One of Mjejane’s hidden gems — a tranquil picnic facility on the buffalo drainage line with abundant birdlife.

Learn more →

Wildlife & Big 5 Sightings

A wide variety of wildlife, including the Big 5, roams freely between the Kruger National Park and the Mjejane Game Reserve.
