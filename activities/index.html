Plan Your Bush Escape

Activities in the Mjejane Game Reserve

Jacana River Lodge Game Drive in the Mjejane Game Reserve

Go on an adventure in our 10-seater game viewing vehicle and experience the most spectacular encounters in the heart of the African Bushveld. Exclusively offered to guests staying at Jacana River Lodge. Learn more

Discover Kruger Private Drives with Ranger Special Khoza

Experience an unforgettable private Kruger safari with Ranger Special Khoza, offering expert-guided game drives and intimate encounters with Africa’s iconic wildlife. Learn more

Unforgettable Kruger Park Safaris with Irving Knight Safaris

Enjoy an unforgettable Kruger National Park safari with Irving Knight Safaris, guided by experienced rangers who will help you discover the Big Five and the incredible wildlife of the African bush. Learn more

Mjejane Game Reserve Bush Walks

Explore Mjejane’s wildlife with 3.5-to-4.5-hour guided bush walks every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. View this activity

Mjejane Bush Spa

Mjejane Mobile Bush Spa not only comes to you with offers of pampering, restorative massages and luxury treatments; the Bush Spa is also deeply rooted in African tradition and themes, and uses the finest locally sourced products. Learn more

Mjejane Birding Guide

A bird guide to some of the sensational avian wildlife that ‘twitchers’ (also known as birders) are constantly on the lookout for in the Mjejane Game Reserve. Learn more

Kingsland Recreational Area

The Kingsland picnic facility is one of Mjejane’s hidden gems. It is situated on the tranquil buffalo drainage line with lots of birdlife and is the perfect place to unwind and relax under the Tamboti trees. Learn more

Mjejane Game Reserve Wildlife

A wide variety of wildlife, including the Big 5, roam freely between the KNP and Mjejane Game Reserve. Therefore, Jacana River Lodge has its own private game viewing vehicle to enjoy the ±4 000 hectares of untouched African bush. Learn more

More Activities

Golf Clubs in the Surrounding Area

Malelane Golf Club

Malelane Golf Club features a stunning 9-hole golf course with beautifully maintained greens. Located only a club swing away from the Kruger National Park and surrounded by indigenous trees, this golf course is not to be missed. Learn more

Kambaku Golf Club & Restaurant

Tee off with the stunning Lebombo Mountains as a backdrop at Kambaku Golf Club. Situated close to the Kruger National Park, its 9-hole course will challenge the skill level of any golfer. Kambaku Golf Club also features a well-stocked bar with a breezy restaurant, the perfect relaxation spot after an exciting golfing experience. Learn more
