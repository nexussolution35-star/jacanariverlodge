Natural game species roam freely between Mjejane’s 4000 ha and the Kruger National Park, especially towards the end of winter when grasslands in the north of the park become sparse. Enjoy wonderful game drives in our 10-seater game viewer in Big 5 country!

A wide variety of wildlife, including the Big 5, roam freely between the KNP and the Mjejane Game Reserve. That is why Jacana River Lodge has its own private game viewing vehicle to enjoy the ±4 000 hectares of untouched African bush.

News from the Game Viewers

Terrific sightings of the Big 5, plains game herds and a variety of beautiful birds are captured daily on game drives on the reserve. Our guests have posted some of their top encounters.

See more sightings

The Mjejane Game Reserve “Population”

Mjejane dam is a popular sundowner spot on the reserve. There is an abundance of birds around the dam, as well as crocodile, elephant, waterbuck and large pods of hippo that watch you as you sip on your favourite G&T.

Mjejane Game Reserve is full of wildlife moving in from the northern parts of the Kruger National Park as the grasslands get scarce towards the end of winter. Look out for these rare species:

  • Aquatic turtles
  • Water monitor
  • African fish eagle
  • All the kingfisher species
  • The famous jacana

Our Lion Pride

Mjejane Private Nature Reserve is home to a large pride of lions. This pride is made up of 3 large males, 6 females and 16 cubs. We have had the privilege of seeing the lions on a weekly basis; the cubs are very playful and even try their luck with the big males sometimes.
