Guest Reviews on TripAdvisor

Five-star reviews from guests on TripAdvisor.

We couldn’t have asked for a more perfect venue for an 80th surprise getaway, only more time… Jacana is a luxurious house with a perfect view of the river and all the amenities one could ask for. One need not leave the house to get a game experience, as the ellies, hippos and crocs are the morning, afternoon and early evening entertainment every day. If you do, ask for William: he was so interesting and knowledgeable, even teaching us that the magic gwarrie is used as a “bush toothbrush”. Sunsets on the deck and nights around the firepit with the bush sounds as our music were magical. Thank you Mariska for assisting us with our booking.

– KIM K, July 13, 2021 · TripAdvisor

More about Guest Reviews on TripAdvisor

Located in the more peaceful area of Mjejane, with superb facilities. It’s very hard to say what I enjoyed the most:

– Watching Kruger from the patio: giraffes, buffalos, hippos, elephants
– Infinity pool
– Beautiful lodge
– Extremely beautiful bedrooms
– Large covered area and sofas outdoors and indoors
– The best amenities: small gym, top kitchen, ice machine, covered and open-air boma, pool table etc.

I strongly encourage you to book this place, you will not be disappointed.

For game viewing: Mjejane has an excellent possibility to see the Big 5, and game drives can be arranged for morning and evening. As they take just a few hours, you can return to the lodge to enjoy the day and go back to game viewing during the evening.

If you would like to explore Kruger, you can go for a full or half day.

– 553villep, February 27, 2020 · TripAdvisor

Went to Jacana lodge early September, and what an experience. No photos or reviews could truly describe the beauty and the atmosphere of this place.

From the amazing view of the Crocodile River, where all manner of animals large and small come to drink water from the river, to the luxurious bedrooms and entertainment areas. I never wanted to leave the upstairs patio where I could drink cocktails and watch the sun go down over the river.

During my visit I went on a game drive and it was definitely the best one I have ever been on. The custom-made game viewer was so comfortable it just made the trip so enjoyable. On our game drive we saw a large number of animals from giraffe to rhino. But my personal favourite was the African elephant: we saw baby elephants running towards their mother as they realised that she had already walked a distance away from them.

– Chade H, November 6, 2019 · TripAdvisor

Cyclone Eloise Brings New Life to the River

We had an awesome time at the lodge! Wildlife was abundant. The venue was stunning and we didn’t have to go anywhere to see animals. The hippos were playing in the river up and down the stream, and waterbuck grazed close by. With Cyclone Eloise bringing so much rain, the Crocodile River was teeming with activity. It was amazing!!

– Anonymous, 15 January 2021

Truly a Five-Star Authentic African Experience

How do you start by reviewing the best place in Africa? Photos certainly do not do justice to the beauty and magnificence of this house. For instance, all the appliances, bedding and furniture are of the best quality and truly understated elegance. You shall want for nothing at Jacana. Small touches, for instance the children’s play area and wheelchair access, make this house feel like a home. The location of Jacana River Lodge makes this a totally private stay. Certainly something that is unheard of so close to the Kruger National Park.

This is where you can sit on the veranda or in the pool and just watch the world go by. Usually with elephants right on your doorstep. During our stay we saw elephants, leopards, rhino and lions, almost on a daily basis. We were easily reminded, in this most special setting, just what a privilege it is to witness these wildlife sightings. Also the lives of some of our most precious natural assets. To sit and watch an African sunset on Jacana’s amazing upstairs entertainment deck has left us with such special memories and family moments to treasure. Lying on a pure white Egyptian cotton bed, looking out over the Crocodile River teeming with wildlife, was a fantasy I only thought possible in films. The amazing setting is matched only by the house itself. Truly a five-star Authentic African Experience.

– Madelien de Wet, 9 December 2019
