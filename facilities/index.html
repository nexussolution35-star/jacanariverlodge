Lodge facilities

Facilities

Everything at the lodge for a self-catering stay near the Kruger National Park, from the fully equipped kitchen to the braai areas, boma and rim-flow pool. Read our self-catering guide for what’s supplied and what to bring.

See the rooms and suites
  1. 01

    1. Veranda, Deck and Pool

    Step out onto a veranda for alfresco dining, or enjoy ultimate relaxation and spectacular views over the river in a separate seating area. Make use of the ice machine and installed service counter, or jump into our blissful rim-flow swimming pool.

  2. 02

    2. Boma and Braais

    Choose between two covered braai (barbecue) areas or the boma. All cooking tools necessary for preparing your favourite meals are available, and firewood is supplied. The upper deck and bar are ideal for hosting memorable get-togethers, and especially for watching breathtaking African sunsets!

  3. 03

    3. Open-Plan Living Areas

    The lounge, dining area and kitchen are spacious, yet have a homely, cosy atmosphere. The kitchen is fully equipped with a double-door fridge/freezer, gas stove, conventional oven, microwave oven, Nespresso machine and more. The lodge is fully serviced with a cable network and Wi-Fi.

  4. 04

    4. 5 En-Suite Bedrooms

    Luxury and comfort await you! Lie back on an impeccably dressed king-size bed, and enjoy the convenience of a bar fridge, coffee station, air-conditioning, ceiling fan, plug points for charging, clock radio and mosquito nets. The lodge is wheelchair-friendly with one bedroom dedicated to guests with special needs. Each en-suite bathroom comprises a toilet, double vanity and shower and/or bath, while some rooms also have an outside shower.

  5. 05

    5. Service Areas

    Additional facilities include a scullery with washing machine, dishwashing machine and extra fridges for use by guests. Jacana River Lodge has everything, and more, to make you feel at home and ensure the best possible stay!

  6. 06

    6. Kiddies’ Playroom and Small Gym

    Our entertainment room will keep the young ones busy! Comfortable seating allows them to watch TV, and enjoy a drawing board, books and games. Also available are a pool table and a small gym, comprising an exercise bike, rowing machine, weights and mat.

  7. 07

    7. Courtyard, Lawn and Jungle Gym

    Inside our courtyard, which is secured by perimeter walls, kids can run around safely on the lawn or enjoy the jungle gym’s swings and slide.

  8. 08

    8. Safe Parking

    Undercover parking for three vehicles, as well as a conveniently situated carport for easy unpacking, and for hopping on and off the game viewer.

Reserve rules and fines

Please note: The reserve is governed by strict rules with regard to respect for animals, other visitors or property, and the owners of Jacana River Lodge expect guests to comply with these rules. The managing body of Mjejane Game Reserve will issue fines for any transgressions on the reserve, such as speeding or reckless driving. Any person guilty of such transgressions will be personally liable for the fines.
