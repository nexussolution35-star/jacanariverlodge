Cookies and Privacy Policy

How Jacana River Lodge collects, uses and protects your personal information, in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Jacana River Lodge (“the Lodge”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you visit jacanariverlodge.com, make an enquiry or booking, or contact us.

We process personal information in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). If you have any questions, please contact us at info@jacanariverlodge.com.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Jacana River Lodge, a self-catering lodge in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve, Hectorspruit, Mpumalanga, South Africa, is the “responsible party” under POPIA. This means we decide why and how your personal information is processed.

  • Responsible party: Jacana River Lodge, Mjejane Private Game Reserve, Hectorspruit, Mpumalanga, South Africa
  • Information Officer: the Lodge Owner’s representative, info@jacanariverlodge.com
  • Telephone: +27 (0)66 293 7100

2. What personal information we collect

Information you give us

When you make an enquiry, book a stay, chat with us or contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp or a form on this website, we may collect:

  • your first name and surname
  • your email address
  • your phone number
  • the details of your enquiry or booking, such as preferred dates, the number of guests in your group and any requests you share with us
  • the messages you send us, including website chat conversations

Information collected automatically

When you visit the website we may collect information on how it is accessed and used (“usage data”). This may include your device’s IP address, browser type and version, the pages you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data. Much of this is collected through cookies and similar technologies (see section 5).

3. How we use your information

We use personal information only for specific, lawful purposes connected to running the Lodge and this website:

  • Enquiries and bookings: to answer your questions, prepare quotes and handle your booking.
  • Guest communication: to contact you about your enquiry or booking before, during and after your stay, and to provide customer care and support.
  • Running the website: to provide and maintain the website, keep it secure, and detect, prevent and fix technical problems.
  • Improving the website: to monitor how the website is used and understand what visitors find useful, so that we can improve it.
  • Advertising: to measure how well our online advertising works and to show relevant adverts (see section 6).
  • Marketing: to send you news and specials, but only if you have agreed to receive them. Every marketing message gives you a simple way to opt out.
  • Legal obligations: to keep the records the law requires and to respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Lawful basis and consent

POPIA allows us to process personal information only where there is a lawful reason to do so. Depending on the purpose, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Your booking: the processing is needed to take steps you have asked for before a booking, or to carry out your booking.
  • Your consent: for example, to send you marketing messages or to set non-essential cookies.
  • Legal obligations: where the law requires us to keep or share certain information.
  • Legitimate interests: where the processing is needed to pursue the Lodge’s legitimate interests, such as keeping the website secure and understanding how it is used, in a way that does not override your rights.

Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time by contacting us at info@jacanariverlodge.com or by using the unsubscribe link in our messages. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it.

5. Cookies and how to control them

Cookies are small files, which may include an anonymous unique identifier, that a website sends to your browser and stores on your device. We and the services we use also rely on similar technologies such as beacons, tags and scripts to collect information and to improve and analyse the website.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential and session cookies to operate the website.
  • Preference cookies to remember your preferences and settings.
  • Security cookies for security purposes.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics and Hotjar) to understand how visitors use the website.
  • Advertising cookies (Google Ads) to measure and personalise our adverts.
  • Functional cookies set by our website chat and enquiry forms.

Controlling cookies

  • You can set your browser to refuse all cookies, to tell you when a cookie is being sent, or to delete cookies already stored. Your browser’s help pages explain how.
  • You can stop Google Analytics from collecting your visits by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • You can manage personalised Google adverts in Google’s ad settings.

If you refuse cookies, some parts of the website, such as the chat or booking tools, may not work properly.

6. Analytics, advertising and tracking tools

We use the following third-party services to understand and improve how the website is used and to manage our advertising:

  • Google Analytics tracks and reports website traffic. Google uses the data collected to monitor use of the website, and this data may be shared with other Google services.
  • Google Ads helps us measure our adverts and show them to people who may be interested. Google may use the data collected to personalise the adverts in its own advertising network.
  • Google Tag Manager lets us manage the tags and scripts used on the website.
  • Hotjar helps us understand how visitors use our pages, for example which links are clicked and how far people scroll, so that we can improve the website.

These tools may group visitors by interests and behaviour (sometimes called profiling) so that we can show more relevant information and adverts. We do not use them to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

For more on how these providers handle data, see the Google Privacy Policy and the Hotjar Privacy Policy.

7. Website chat and AI-assisted replies

An AI-assisted chat widget may be used on this website to answer common questions, for example about the Lodge, availability or activities, and to pass your enquiry on to us.

  • Chat conversations, and any contact details you share in them, may be stored by our CRM provider so that we can follow up on your enquiry.
  • AI-generated answers may not always be complete or accurate. Please confirm important details, such as rates and availability, with us directly.
  • Please do not share sensitive information, such as identity numbers or card details, in the chat.

8. Who we share your information with

We use trusted service providers (“operators” under POPIA) to run the website and handle enquiries and bookings:

  • GoHighLevel, our customer relationship management (CRM) system, which runs our website forms and chat and stores enquiry records.
  • NightsBridge, our online booking engine, which captures the details of bookings made online.
  • Google (Google Analytics, Google Ads and Google Tag Manager), for analytics and advertising.
  • Hotjar, for website behaviour analytics.
  • Our email and website hosting providers.

Operators may use your personal information only to perform these tasks on our behalf. They must keep it confidential and secure, and may not use or disclose it for any other purpose.

Legal requirements

We may also disclose personal information if we believe in good faith that it is necessary to:

  • comply with a legal obligation
  • protect and defend the rights or property of Jacana River Lodge
  • prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the website
  • protect the personal safety of website users, guests or the public
  • protect against legal liability

9. Cross-border transfers

Some of our service providers, including our CRM, email, analytics and advertising providers, may store or process personal information on servers outside South Africa.

We only allow such transfers where POPIA permits them, for example where the recipient is bound by laws, rules or an agreement that give a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA, or where the transfer is needed for your booking. We take reasonable steps to make sure that adequate controls, including security, are in place.

If you are located outside South Africa and contact or book with us, your information will be transferred to and processed in South Africa.

10. How long we keep your information

  • We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, or for as long as the law requires (for example, for tax and accounting records).
  • Marketing preferences are kept until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent.
  • Website usage data is kept in line with the retention settings of the analytics service concerned.

When we no longer need personal information, we delete or destroy it, or de-identify it so that it can no longer be linked to you.

11. How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised access and unlawful processing. These include:

  • limiting access to the people who need it to handle your enquiry or booking
  • protecting accounts and systems with passwords and verification
  • using reputable service providers that are required to keep information secure

If we have reasonable grounds to believe that your personal information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify the Information Regulator and you as soon as reasonably possible, as POPIA requires, and take steps to contain the breach, such as changing passwords.

No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so while we work hard to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

12. Your rights under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access: ask whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy of it.
  • Correction: ask us to correct or update information that is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete or destroy information that we are no longer allowed to keep.
  • Objection: object to us processing your information on reasonable grounds, and object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent you have given us at any time.
  • Complain: lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To use any of these rights, email info@jacanariverlodge.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on your request. Some information may have to be kept for legal, administrative or security reasons.

The Information Regulator

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal information, please let us know first so that we can try to put it right. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa:

  • Website: inforeg.org.za
  • Complaints: complaints.IR@inforeg.org.za

13. Children’s information

This website is not aimed at children (anyone under 18), and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

When a parent or guardian books a stay, they may give us limited information about children in the group, such as the number of children travelling. We use this only to handle the booking.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information without your consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

14. Links to other websites

This website contains links to, and may include content embedded from, other websites, such as activity operators, social media platforms and map or video services. Embedded content behaves as if you had visited the other website, which may collect data about you, use cookies and track your interaction with it.

We have no control over, and are not responsible for, the content, privacy policies or practices of third-party websites. Please read the privacy policy of each website you visit.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. Where a change is significant, we will also take reasonable steps to let you know, for example with a notice on the website.

Please review this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
