Directions to Jacana River Lodge

In the Mjejane Private Game Reserve near Hectorspruit, Mpumalanga, on the banks of the Crocodile River.

Getting here

On the Southern Boundary of the Kruger

The Mjejane Game Reserve sits on the Kruger National Park’s southern boundary, between the Malelane and Crocodile Bridge gates, near Hectorspruit (Emjejane). Hectorspruit lies between Malelane and Komatipoort.

  • By road from Johannesburg: about four hours’ drive.
  • By air: about an hour’s drive from Kruger Mpumalanga International Airport (KMIA).
  • At the reserve gate: Mjejane charges a conservation fee of R350 per vehicle (seven-day cycle) and R60 per guest per night, payable on arrival at the main gate.

Full directions to the lodge are sent with your booking confirmation. If you need help on the way, call or WhatsApp us.
