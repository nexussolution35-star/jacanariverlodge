In the Mjejane Game Reserve

Private Game Drives

Go on an adventure in our 10-seater game viewing vehicle and experience the most spectacular encounters in the heart of the African Bushveld. Game drives in the Mjejane Game Reserve are known to be filled with action and more photo opportunities than you can ever imagine. A large variety of wildlife, including the world-renowned Big 5, roam freely between the Kruger National Park and the Mjejane Game Reserve.

The Mjejane Game Reserve is a one-of-a-kind destination. As part of the Greater Kruger concession area, the Mjejane is a special place. The 4 000 hectares that make up the game reserve are renowned for being the best for wildlife viewing, and one of the best ways to take in all the sights and sounds, while breathing in that pure Bushveld air, is a half-day game drive.

More about Private Game Drives

Whether it is a morning spent out on the open plains of the Mjejane Game Reserve, or a sunset game drive along the banks of the Crocodile River, a guided drive is one of those experiences that everyone visiting this corner of the world should book.

At Jacana River Lodge we provide our guests with an unforgettable stay. At our luxurious, family-friendly accommodation you can look forward to plenty of regenerating downtime, and in the peace and quiet of the Mjejane Private Game Reserve you can enjoy exclusive wildlife sightings, well away from the crowds that frequent other nearby nature reserves.

Mjejane Game Reserve Safaris

Although other parks in the area, such as the Kruger, also offer half-day game drives, a Mjejane Game Reserve safari is a unique experience. Taking place in some of the best wildlife viewing areas, you are sure to see plenty of extraordinary wildlife and leave the game drive having had a fulfilling safari experience.

Just a morning or an afternoon out on safari (usually the amount of time considered to be a half-day game drive) is enough time to see the best of what the reserve has to offer. The Mjejane is home to the famous Big 5 and all sorts of unusual critters.

More about Mjejane Game Reserve Safaris

The benefit of a guided game drive is that you can sit back and relax. You don’t have to keep your eyes on the road or worry about getting lost along the way. The guides who take you on your Mjejane Game Reserve safari are experienced and know how to treat guests to a most enjoyable time, and with their immense knowledge you can learn more about what you see.

Mjejane Game Reserve safaris take place in a proper safari vehicle, giving you maximum viewing potential while letting you really take in the atmosphere the reserve is so well known for. A guided safari is a great way to bond with your family and friends: seated in an open safari vehicle, you share in the excitement of every sighting, and the experience will be worth chatting about for hours afterwards.
