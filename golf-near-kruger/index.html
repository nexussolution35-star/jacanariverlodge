Golf near the Kruger: Kambaku Golf Club and Malelane Golf Club

Two 9-hole courses close to the Kruger National Park, an easy outing from Jacana River Lodge in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve.

Golf clubs in the surrounding area

Golf near the Kruger

Combine a stay in the bush with a round of golf. Jacana River Lodge is in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve on the Kruger National Park’s southern boundary, about 38 minutes’ drive from Malelane, and there are two 9-hole golf clubs in the surrounding area.

Kambaku9 holes, bar and restaurant
BackdropThe Lebombo Mountains
Malelane9 holes, indigenous trees
Malelane hours7 days, 7:00am to 4:30pm
From the lodgeAbout 38 minutes to Malelane

Kambaku Golf Club & Restaurant

Tee off with the stunning Lebombo Mountains as a backdrop at Kambaku Golf Club. Situated close to the Kruger National Park, its 9-hole course will challenge the skill level of any golfer. Kambaku Golf Club also features a well-stocked bar with a breezy restaurant, the perfect relaxation spot after an exciting round.

For tee times and more information, visit the Kambaku Golf Club website, or read our post on the Kambaku Golf Club & Restaurant.

Malelane Golf Club

Malelane Golf Club features a stunning 9-hole golf course with beautifully maintained greens. Located only a club swing away from the Kruger National Park and surrounded by indigenous trees, this golf course is not to be missed.

  • Open: 7 days a week, 7:00am to 4:30pm
  • Email: malgc@lantic.net
  • Phone: (013) 790 0283
  • Green fees: Malelane Golf Club membership and green fees (PDF)

Contact the club directly to book a round, or read our post on the Malelane Golf Club.
