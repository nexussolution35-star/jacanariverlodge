Our guided bush treks offer an unparalleled opportunity to immerse yourself in the beauty of nature on foot, with an intimate encounter with wildlife in its natural habitat.

Immerse Yourself in the Wilderness

A walking safari in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve is a 3.5-to-4.5-hour hike that places you in the heart of the African bush. Our seasoned guides lead weekly walks every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, giving you the chance to witness wildlife up close and personal. The guided bush treks are priced at R950 per person, including transportation to and from the walking area.

A Distinct Perspective

While a traditional game drive is exhilarating, nothing compares to the serene intimacy of encountering wildlife on foot. Stroll through an area untouched by human interference and connect with nature on a profound level. Free from exhaust fumes and running engines, you can genuinely appreciate the sights and sounds of the wilderness.

Trail Highlights
  • Duration: maximum 4 hours
  • Departure days: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday
  • Departure time: 05:30
  • Cost: R950 per person

Guiding Principles
  • Tailored for outdoor enthusiasts
  • Trails led by experienced guides prioritising your safety
  • Following your guide’s instructions is crucial
  • Stay hydrated, especially during the hot Lowveld summers
  • Tick deterrents are recommended at certain times of the year
  • Gaiters are advised for protection against ticks, thorns and burs

Transport Details

Arrange your transport to the departure point at River Lodge, behind the two Wendy houses. All participants must meet at the departure point at 05:30.

Who Can Take Part
  • No maximum age restriction, but participants should be reasonably fit
  • Unaccompanied children must be at least sixteen (16) years old
  • Children under twelve (12) years may not take part
  • All participants must sign an indemnity form
  • Minors need a letter of consent from a parent or legal guardian

Dress Code

Blend in with the surroundings by wearing suitable colours like khaki and green. Avoid bright colours like white, red, yellow, orange and pink; dark colours are preferable to light colours.

Equipment Provided & What to Bring
  • Provided: first aid kit, brown paper bag, shovel
  • Bring: ample water, snacks, hat, sunblock, camera and binoculars

Booked with SANParks

SANParks Walking Safaris in the Kruger National Park

Why walk when I can drive?

Walking is an eco-friendly way to interact with nature, with minimal impact on the environment. The idea is to see without being seen, and so not to alter the behaviour of any animals encountered along the way. You leave the roads behind and get off the beaten track.

Out in the open your senses are on high alert, immersing you in the smells and sounds around you. A walk offers an intimate glimpse into a world of trees, tracks, insects, reptiles and other small animals. You can go where vehicles can’t and follow ancient animal paths. You’ll hear fascinating alarm calls, get a real sense of adventure and exploration, and take in so much more detail than in a car.

A walking safari is not about ticking off the Big 5 from a list, but rather about noticing the little things. Tracks, droppings, pods, seeds, animal calls and smells take on new meaning and help you interpret the rugged, unspoilt landscape around you.

Will I be safe?

Animals associate humans with danger and will move away if approached. Experienced guides, armed in the event of real peril, use their senses and knowledge of animal tracks to get you as close as possible to wildlife while keeping everyone out of harm’s way. The emphasis is on a safe and respectful experience, where guests’ comfort and wellbeing are of paramount importance, and animals and the environment are left unhindered.

Can everyone take part?

Unfortunately, these walking safaris exclude children younger than 12, and persons older than 65 need to present a doctor’s letter confirming fitness. Although the walk is not strenuous, it lasts 4–5 hours, so participants should be in good health and able to withstand a fair amount of physical exertion.

When and where?

Guided bush walks take place early in the morning on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Your game ranger will drive you to one of the walking blocks in an open safari vehicle. After a safety briefing, you set off on foot. Times and details are confirmed at the time of booking.

Costs and requirements for Kruger National Park bush walks

  • R550 per person for 4-6 people
  • R500 per person for 5-6 people
  • R450 per person for 7-8 people

Wear comfortable clothes and closed walking shoes in neutral colours, and don’t forget your hat and sunscreen. Bring a small backpack for snacks and water (we provide the water), a camera and binoculars.

You can book with the KNP online at www.sanparks.org, phone them on 012 428 9111 or email reservations@sanparks.org.
