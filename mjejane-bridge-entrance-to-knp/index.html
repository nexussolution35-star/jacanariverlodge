Into the Kruger National Park

Mjejane Game Reserve Bridge Entrance to Kruger National Park

Full or Half-DayTrips
5 StarRating on TripAdvisor
EndlessPhoto Opportunities

Drive comfortably in your own vehicle (high ground clearance required) or hire the Jacana River Lodge game viewer (full or half-day) and take a trip to the Kruger National Park via the Mjejane Game Reserve Bridge.

NB: Jacana River Lodge guests only.
View Rates & Booking Procedure KNP Bridge Booking Form

Game Viewing Time Slots

From 07:00 or from 16:00

Jacana River Lodge has two available game viewing time slots that you can choose from. Each of these time slots will provide a totally unique game viewing experience.

What You Should Bring

There are a few items that everyone should bring along on their game drive to ensure an amazing experience. Here are a few:

  • Binoculars
  • Bird book
  • Hat
  • Camera
  • Mammal book
  • Sun block
  • Reptile book
