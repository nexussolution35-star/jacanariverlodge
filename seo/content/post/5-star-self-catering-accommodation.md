# 5 star self catering accommodation

![Jacana River Lodge is a five star Self catering accommodation for a family of up to 10 members.](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/TGCSA_5_star-pdf-01-e1569395048187.png)

### What makes a lodge 5-star self catering accommodation?

#### It is all in the finishes

A 5-star self catering accommodation has all the bells and whistles. Yes, it is only reserved for the most luxurious accommodation in South Africa. The recognition of exceptional 5-star properties consists of exclusive use of the lodge, en-suite bathroom facilities, and onsite game lodge manager and more. In this article you will find why Jacana River Lodge received a 5-star grading as a self catering accommodation [facility](https://jacanariverlodge.com/facilities/).

![5-star Self Catering Accommodation, children's play area in Jacana River Lodge](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/IMG_4777-1024x683.jpg)

#### Safety for the whole family

At Jacana River Lodge we have covered parking facilities that are well-lit. With ample space to fit a fully equipped 4×4 vehicle. For your family’s safety, a high voltage electric fence protects the lodge. We built the lodge to include an enclosed outdoor play area for children in the courtyard. So you can relax with the knowledge that everyone is happy and safe at the lodge. Especially while the African big-5 are grazing and hunting on the edge of your doorstep. If anything unforeseen should happen, emergency information is available here on the website and at the lodge in various places.

#### Bedrooms

Firstly, you want to feel save when you’re asleep. So, all our bedroom doors are lockable, with a secondary guest-controlled internal lock and peephole. This lock can only be opened from the inside. Sliding doors lead out into the garden, patio or courtyard. Every room has a small safe to store your valuables.

Secondly, you want to sleep comfortably. Our king-size beds are big enough to fit taller people too. Also, these beds can split. So, every child may have their own bed to sleep in. This will certainly keep the peace between siblings.

Thirdly, we use mattress and pillow protectors on all our beds. This ensures that every bed and pillow stays clean. And, above all, our guest can enjoy a more refreshing sleeping experience while on holiday.

If the nights get a bit chilly, we have an additional blanket in the cupboard. The blanket is hygienically sealed in a plastic bag.

There are also more than enough pillows to go around. Especially if you sleep better when you hug something soft and fluffy. Or, you may need a special-requirement pillow for medical reasons. We made provision for that too! Your comfort is our priority.

#### Bedroom Furniture

All beds have bedside tables with bed lights. So, you have some place to put your cellphone, glasses or book. Anything that you may need during the evening. Each room has at least one comfortable chair. In addition, every room has a dressing table with its own chair and fitted with a large mirror.

Cupboards have enough hanging space, even for full length dresses and jackets. There are enough shelves and drawers to live comfortably for an extended holiday.

The curtains in each bedroom is are thick enough to block out all the sunlight. So you can sleep in whenever you like.

Most of all, there is a mini-bar fridge, hair dryer, and coffee station in every bedroom. And, free uncapped WiFi throughout the lodge.
