# Top Private Concessions in Kruger National Park for 2024

## 1. Private Concessions Unveiled

Embark on an extraordinary safari adventure as Kruger National Park's private concessions unveil a world of exclusivity and wildlife wonders. [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/), spanning 4000 hectares, is a standout destination, offering a unique blend of freedom and flexibility for families and friends.

### Flexibility and Freedom:

In Mjejane, the untamed beauty of the African bush unfolds as families and friends enjoy dedicated vehicles and guides, setting their own pace in the vast landscapes teeming with rich biodiversity. The freedom to tailor the safari experience enhances the adventure, making it a perfect escape.

### Exclusive Night Safaris and Bushwalks:

As the sun sets over Mjejane, the magic of the bush comes alive during exclusive night safaris. Explore the nocturnal wonders and indulge in guided bushwalks led by experienced rangers, providing an intimate connection with nature, perfect for families seeking adventure.

### Uncrowded Luxury:

Mjejane distinguishes itself with limited vehicles, ensuring an uncrowded and exclusive safari experience. Families and large groups can relish the tranquility of the bush, immersed in the sights and sounds of nature without the distraction of crowded safari vehicles.

## 2. The Self-Catering 5-Star Experience at Jacana River Lodge

Nestled in the heart of Mjejane, [Jacana River Lodge](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/) offers a self-catering 5-star experience, redefining luxury in the midst of the African bush.

### Nature's Spectacle at Mjejane Dam:

Jacana River Lodge provides breathtaking views of Mjejane dam, a popular sundowner spot. The dam is surrounded by an abundance of birds, including Crocodile, Elephant, Waterbuck, and large pods of Hippo, creating a mesmerizing backdrop for enjoying a refreshing G&T.

### Magnificent Wildlife Encounters:

Mjejane Game Reserve is a haven for wildlife, with species migrating from the Northern parts of Kruger National Park as winter progresses. Look out for rare species such as Aquatic Turtles, Water Monitor, Africa Fish Eagle, various Kingfisher species, and the iconic Jacana.

### Meet the Lion Pride:

Mjejane Game Reserve proudly hosts a large pride of Lions—3 large males, 6 females, and 16 playful cubs. Witness their playful antics, including cubs challenging each other and occasionally trying their luck with the big males.

### Verreaux Eagle Owls:

Encounter the majestic Verreaux Eagle Owls, residents of Mjejane Nature Reserve, and witness the growth of their nearly full-grown chicks. Learn about their distinctive features, such as ear tufts for enhanced hearing, and appreciate the family dynamics as the chicks venture out in search of food.

### Guide to Mjejane Birding:

Explore the sensational avian wildlife of Mjejane Game Reserve through the expert insights of safari guides. With over 500 bird species, including special treasures unique to Mjejane, birders can delight in the diverse birdlife within the Kruger National Park.

## 3. Family-Friendly Safari Activities

Mjejane Game Reserve ensures a family-friendly approach to safari activities, catering to diverse interests and age groups.

### Flexibility for All Ages:

Specially crafted game drives cater to the diverse interests of all family members. Younger children can engage in interactive learning experiences, while teenagers and adults can delve into more in-depth wildlife encounters.

### Bushwalks for the Adventurous:

For families with a taste for adventure, guided bushwalks are a must. Led by experienced rangers, these walks provide a different perspective on the African bush. It's an opportunity to learn about tracking, plant life, and the smaller inhabitants of the reserve.

### Educational Programs for Young Explorers:

Mjejane Game Reserve goes the extra mile to make safaris educational and entertaining for younger visitors. Engaging programs introduce them to the wonders of nature, fostering a lifelong appreciation for wildlife conservation.
