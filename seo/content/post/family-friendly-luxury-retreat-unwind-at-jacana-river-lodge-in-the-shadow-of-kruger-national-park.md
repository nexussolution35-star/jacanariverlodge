# Family-Friendly Luxury Retreat: Unwind at Jacana River Lodge in the Shadow of Kruger National Park

![IMG_8190](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/IMG_8190.jpg)

Escape the hustle and bustle of everyday life and treat your family to a luxurious retreat at [Jacana River Lodge](../), nestled within the scenic [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/) near Kruger National Park. With its unparalleled amenities and serene surroundings, our lodge promises a truly unforgettable experience for families of all ages.

Indulge in ultimate relaxation on the veranda, deck, and pool area, where you can soak up the sun, enjoy alfresco dining, or simply unwind with a refreshing drink while taking in the breathtaking views of the river and surrounding wilderness.

For a taste of authentic African cuisine, head to the boma or one of the covered braai areas, where you can cook up a storm using the provided cooking tools and firewood. The upper deck and bar offer a picturesque setting for watching African sunsets and hosting memorable gatherings with your loved ones.

With self-catering facilities available throughout the lodge, you have the freedom to create delicious meals in the fully equipped kitchen, complete with modern appliances and conveniences. Stay connected with cable network and Wi-Fi throughout the lodge, ensuring you never miss a moment of relaxation or adventure.

Join us at Jacana River Lodge for a family-friendly luxury retreat, where indulgence and adventure await amidst the beauty of the African bush.
