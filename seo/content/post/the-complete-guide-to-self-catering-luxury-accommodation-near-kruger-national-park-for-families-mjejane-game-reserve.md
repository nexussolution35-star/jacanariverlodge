### Why Mjejane Game Reserve Stands Out for Families

Mjejane Game Reserve sits on the southern boundary of Kruger National Park and forms part of the Greater Kruger ecosystem. Because it is a private reserve with low visitor numbers, families experience a quieter, more intimate safari than inside the main park.

The Crocodile River creates a natural magnet for wildlife. Guests regularly see elephants, buffalo, hippos, and other game from the comfort of their lodge. This lodge-based viewing is especially valuable for families with young children or older relatives who prefer not to spend every day on long game drives.

Combined with easy road access from Gauteng (approximately four hours from Johannesburg or Pretoria), Mjejane Game Reserve has become one of the strongest locations for high-quality Mpumalanga accommodation focused on family groups.
