# Family Safari Adventures: Exploring the Borderlands of Kruger National Park at Jacana River Lodge

![Outdoor play area for children, Mjejane game reserve.](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/IMG_5096.jpg)

Embark on a journey of discovery with your family at [Jacana River Lodge](../), nestled within the picturesque [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/), bordering the iconic Kruger National Park. Offering a haven for wildlife enthusiasts and adventure seekers alike, Jacana River Lodge promises an unforgettable safari experience tailored to families.

## A Retreat Unlike Any Other

As you step out onto the expansive veranda, you're greeted by breathtaking views of the river, where alfresco dining and relaxation await. Take advantage of the installed service counter and ice machine for added convenience, or plunge into our blissful rim-flow swimming pool, perfect for cooling off after a day of exploration.

For a taste of authentic African cuisine, head to the boma or one of the covered braai areas, where all the necessary cooking tools are provided, along with complimentary firewood. Alternatively, gather on the upper deck and bar to witness awe-inspiring African sunsets while enjoying refreshing beverages with your loved ones.

Our lodge facilities cater to every need, with additional amenities including a scullery with washing and dishwashing machines, extra fridges, and safe parking for your vehicles. Families can rest assured knowing that our courtyard is secured, allowing children to play freely on the lawn or enjoy the jungle gym's swings and slide.

Inside, our entertainment room offers endless fun for the young ones, featuring comfortable seating, a TV, drawing board, books, games, pool table, and small gym equipment. Each of our five en-suite bedrooms exudes luxury and comfort, with impeccably dressed king-size beds, air-conditioning, and mosquito nets.

The open-plan living areas provide a cozy atmosphere for families to gather, with a fully equipped kitchen boasting modern appliances and conveniences. Stay connected with cable network and Wi-Fi throughout the lodge, ensuring you never miss a moment of relaxation or adventure.

Join us at Jacana River Lodge for an enriching family safari adventure, where cherished memories are made amidst the splendor of the African wilderness.
