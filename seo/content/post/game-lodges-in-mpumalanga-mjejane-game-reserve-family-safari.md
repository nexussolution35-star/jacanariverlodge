# Game Lodges in Mpumalanga: The Big Family Safari in Mjejane Game Reserve

Some safari holidays are made for two. Others are better when the whole family comes along. For families and groups searching for **game lodges in Mpumalanga**, the challenge is finding somewhere that offers enough space to be together without making everyone live on top of one another. That is where an exclusive-use, self-catering lodge can change the way a group experiences the bush.

Set in **Mjejane Game Reserve** on the Crocodile River, [Jacana River Lodge](../) was created with families and larger groups in mind. The five-star self-catering lodge accommodates up to 10 guests in five en-suite bedrooms and is booked for exclusive use, giving one group the lodge as its own private safari base.

For grandparents, parents, children, siblings or friends travelling together, it creates a simple idea for a memorable holiday: bring the people you actually want to spend time with and experience the bush together.

## The Rise of the Big Family Safari

There is something different about travelling as a group. A safari stops being only a collection of individual activities and becomes a shared experience. Breakfast conversations turn into plans for the day. Wildlife sightings become stories everyone retells later. An afternoon around the lodge can be just as much part of the holiday as the time spent exploring the reserve.

That also means accommodation matters.

A larger group needs both communal space and private space. Jacana River Lodge offers five en-suite bedrooms for up to 10 guests, while its open living areas provide places where everyone can come back together. Instead of separating a family across different hotel rooms, the lodge provides one home base for the group.

Two of Jacana's bedrooms face the Crocodile River, while the lodge itself enjoys a riverfront position within Mjejane Game Reserve.

## Why Exclusive-Use Self-Catering Works for Families

Self-catering gives a group something especially valuable on holiday: flexibility.

There is no need for every member of the family to want breakfast at exactly the same time or to structure every meal around a restaurant schedule. Guests have access to a fully equipped kitchen and can organise meals around their own plans.

For a family with children, grandparents or several adults travelling together, that freedom can make the holiday feel considerably more relaxed.

Exclusive use adds another dimension. Jacana is booked as a complete lodge rather than shared between unrelated groups. Spaces such as the lounge, deck, swimming pool and boma become part of your group's own stay.

You can explore more of what is included on Jacana's [self-catering accommodation facilities page](../facilities/).

## Five Bedrooms Means Space to Be Together — and Apart

The best group holidays usually contain both.

Everyone wants the evenings when the family gathers together, but people also need somewhere to retreat when they want a quieter hour. Five en-suite bedrooms give guests private sleeping and bathroom space while keeping everyone under one roof.

Jacana's communal areas then become the meeting point.

There is a double-volume lounge, a wrap-around viewing deck, a rim-flow swimming pool, a boma and outdoor areas where a group can spend time together. Younger visitors also have a dedicated kiddies' playroom and an outdoor jungle gym.

It is one reason the lodge lends itself naturally to an extended-family holiday or a trip involving more than one household.

## A Safari That Doesn't End When the Game Drive Ends

Jacana River Lodge is positioned on the Crocodile River within Mjejane Game Reserve. That setting means the surrounding bush remains part of the experience even when guests are spending time at the lodge.

The viewing deck and river-facing spaces give guests somewhere to slow down, look out across the landscape and enjoy being in the reserve rather than treating the lodge simply as somewhere to sleep between activities.

When it is time to head out, Jacana guests can also arrange a [game drive in Mjejane Game Reserve](../game-drive-mjejane-game-reserve/). Jacana's game-viewing vehicle seats up to 10 guests, meaning a group staying at the lodge can experience a guided drive together.

Mjejane forms part of the Kruger concession area, and Jacana states that wildlife including the Big Five moves between Kruger National Park and Mjejane Game Reserve.

## And Then There Is Kruger National Park

Families who want to extend their safari beyond Mjejane have another option.

Jacana guests can book [full-day or half-day game drives in Kruger National Park](../full-and-half-day-game-drive-in-the-kruger-park/) through the lodge. The lodge also provides information for guests wanting to access Kruger using Mjejane's private bridge across the Crocodile River.

This makes it possible to build a stay around different kinds of days: time exploring Mjejane, a Kruger outing, and slower periods spent enjoying the lodge itself.

For a family holiday, that variety matters. Not every hour has to be programmed for the trip to feel worthwhile.
