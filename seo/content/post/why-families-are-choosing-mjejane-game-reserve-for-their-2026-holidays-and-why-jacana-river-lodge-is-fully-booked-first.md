But the real magic is the wildlife. With permeable boundaries to Kruger, animals roam freely—meaning guests enjoy genuine Big 5 sightings without the crowds. For parents, this means simpler planning, safer environments, and incredible sightings right from the lodge deck.

* No long game drives needed for wildlife encounters

* A gentle environment for young children

It’s everything you want from Kruger—without the overwhelm.

## School Holidays Are the Best Time to Visit—Here’s Why

The school holidays from June to July and again in December are among the best times to experience Mjejane. Wildlife is active, the weather is pleasant, and travel is easier for children. This makes Jacana an ideal base for multi-night family trips.

During the winter holidays:

* Mornings are crisp and perfect for spotting predators
* Midday is warm enough for swimming and lounging
* Visibility is excellent thanks to thinner winter bush
* Sunsets over the Crocodile River are spectacular

During the December holidays:

* Long, warm days allow for flexible game drives
* The bush is vibrant and green after early rains
* Birdlife is at its seasonal peak
* The river is busy with elephants, hippos, and antelope

It’s no wonder Jacana’s holiday periods often fill up 8–12 months in advance.
