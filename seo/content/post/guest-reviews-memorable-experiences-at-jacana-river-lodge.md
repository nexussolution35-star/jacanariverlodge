# Guest Reviews: Memorable Experiences at Jacana River Lodge

![ultimate guide kruger safaris (4)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/ultimate-guide-kruger-safaris-4.jpg)

Jacana River Lodge, nestled in the serene [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/), has been the backdrop for countless unforgettable experiences. Visitors frequently share their tales of adventure, relaxation, and wonder. Here are some heartwarming and exhilarating stories from our guests, reflecting the magic of Jacana River Lodge.

## A Family's Riverside Retreat

Kellie M. shared her delightful experience on TripAdvisor: "Gorgeous lodge perfect for a large family right on the Crocodile River! We loved our stay. The pool was a highlight for everybody. The rooms are gorgeous, especially the front two river suites."

At Jacana River Lodge, families enjoy luxurious accommodations, stunning views of the Crocodile River, and a range of amenities designed for comfort and enjoyment. The lodge's pool offers a refreshing escape, while the beautifully designed riverfront suites provide a perfect blend of elegance and comfort.

## Birthday Celebrations Amidst Wildlife

Nolda Potgieter’s review on Google captures the essence of a celebratory weekend: "We had the most amazing birthday celebrations weekend here at Jacana. From the moment you enter [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/), the service was 10/10. At the lodge, you are met with beautiful views over the river and we enjoyed breathtaking sunsets from the pool and wooden deck. We were entertained by two brother elephants playing the whole day just like boys do. Sometimes rough and then suddenly all good, walking side by side down to the river for a drink. Had the most amazing night drive, following two lions for a long time. This trip was filled with anxiety and pure wonder. Memorable indeed!"

The lodge boasts stunning river views, breathtaking sunsets, and thrilling wildlife sightings right from the deck. Guests can relax in the rim-flow swimming pool, enjoy meals in the alfresco dining area, or unwind in the comfortable outdoor seating. The lodge’s luxurious rooms, equipped with air conditioning and outdoor showers, ensure a five-star experience.

The variety of wildlife in [Mjejane](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/) is truly remarkable. Nolda’s experience of watching elephants play and lions on a night drive are just a glimpse of what the reserve has to offer. [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/) is home to the Big Five (elephant, lion, leopard, buffalo, and rhino), offering guests frequent sightings of these majestic animals. Additionally, the reserve boasts a rich array of other wildlife, including giraffes, zebras, hippos, and numerous antelope species, providing an authentic African safari experience.

## A Family Holiday to Remember

Carmen Chummie’s family holiday was marked by spacious accommodations and excellent client service. She shared on Google: "Absolutely gorgeous and great getaway. Generator available for load shedding. Very spacious. Great client service by Mariska. Thandiwe & Jomo take great care in ensuring a clean and tidy atmosphere."

Jacana River Lodge offers a range of facilities for a comfortable stay, including a generator for uninterrupted power, spacious living areas, and dedicated staff to ensure a clean and welcoming environment. The lodge’s kitchen is fully equipped with modern appliances, making self-catering a breeze.

## A Hidden Gem in Mjejane

Ville Partanen described Jacana River Lodge as a hidden gem: "Located at the more peaceful area of Mjejane with superb facilities. It's very hard to say what I enjoyed the most - watching Kruger from the patio: giraffes, buffalos, hippos, elephants. Infinity pool, beautiful lodge, extremely beautiful bedrooms, large covered area and sofas outdoors and indoors, the best amenities: small gym, top kitchen, ice machine, covered and open-air boma, pool table, etc. I strongly encourage booking this place; you will not be disappointed."

The lodge’s amenities include a small gym, covered and open-air boma, and a pool table, ensuring guests have everything they need for an enjoyable stay. The spacious outdoor and indoor areas provide ample space for relaxation and socializing, while the infinity pool offers a perfect spot to cool off and enjoy the views.

## Breakfast with Elephants

Varvana Myllärniemi shared her unique experience: "We stayed at Jacana River Lodge for four nights in February. Both the location and facilities were outstanding. You could have your breakfast on the terrace while watching elephants bathing in the river. The facilities include outdoor showers, a fireplace on the terrace, air-conditioned small gym, ice machine, infinity pool to the riverside, and of course, a top-notch kitchen equipped for 10 people."

Guests can enjoy their meals on the terrace with stunning views of the river and its wildlife. The lodge’s well-equipped kitchen, outdoor showers, and air-conditioned gym provide a luxurious and comfortable stay, even during the hot and humid months.

## Family Fun by the River

Semona Diener’s family holiday was filled with joy and adventure: "What an amazing experience. Beautiful accommodation with great entertainment areas and swimming pool looking over the river where the hippos bathe. The game drives were excellent and lots to see. Would highly recommend Jacana River Lodge."

Jacana River Lodge offers excellent game viewing opportunities with game drives in [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/). The lodge’s swimming pool and entertainment areas provide perfect spots for relaxation and fun, while the stunning views of the river and its wildlife add to the overall experience.

[Game lodges Mpumalanga](../)’s biodiversity ensures that every game drive is a unique adventure. From spotting the elusive leopard to witnessing large herds of buffalo, guests are treated to an array of wildlife encounters. The reserve’s location along the Crocodile River adds another dimension to the safari experience, with regular sightings of hippos and crocodiles, as well as a variety of bird species that make the riverbanks their home.

The stories shared by our guests highlight the unique and magical experiences at Jacana River Lodge.
