## 6 Reasons to Book Your June 2026 Family Safari at Jacana River Lodge Now

June is one of the most sought-after months for a winter safari in Mpumalanga. If you’re considering self-catering luxury accommodation near Kruger Park, now is the perfect time to secure your booking at Jacana River Lodge in Mjejane Game Reserve before the best dates are taken.

### 1. Outstanding Wildlife Viewing

June marks the beginning of the dry season. Vegetation thins out and animals concentrate around the Crocodile River, delivering excellent Big 5 sightings. Many guests enjoy game viewing directly from the infinity pool and deck.

### 2. Perfect Winter Weather

Enjoy warm, sunny days (22–28°C) and cool evenings — ideal for morning game drives, afternoon pool sessions, and magical boma braais. This comfortable weather makes June especially family-friendly.

### 3. Great Value on Self-Catering Luxury

June offers better pricing than peak December and January periods. You get a full exclusive 5-star private lodge (sleeping up to 10) with all its facilities at more attractive rates — excellent value for multi-generational family groups.

### 4. Complete Privacy and Flexibility

Jacana River Lodge is booked exclusively for one group only. Enjoy your own infinity pool with river views, spacious kitchen, kiddies playroom, jungle gym, and covered boma without sharing with other guests. Set your own relaxed schedule.

### 5. Child-Friendly Luxury

The dedicated playroom, jungle gym, and secure grounds give children space to play safely while parents relax. Wheelchair-friendly room and open-plan design make it comfortable for grandparents too.

### 6. Unforgettable Family Memories

June evenings around the boma fire, pool days with passing elephants, and relaxed self-catering dinners create the kind of special moments families treasure. This is the winter safari experience many people dream about.
