###### 10 Memorable Moments You’ll Only Have at Jacana River Lodge in Mjejane Game Reserve

![Jacana River Lodge pool and view - Game Lodges in Mpumalanga - Jacana River Lodge](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Jacana-River-Lodge-778x480.jpg)![mjejane game reserve accommodation near kruger national park](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-near-kruger-national-park.jpg)

## 10 Memorable Moments You’ll Only Have at Jacana River Lodge in Mjejane Game Reserve

Some safari lodges are beautiful. Others are well located. But **Jacana River Lodge** in the exclusive **Mjejane Game Reserve** offers something far more rare: a collection of unforgettable moments that happen only when your lodge is positioned directly on the Crocodile River, surrounded by wildlife, and reserved exclusively for one group at a time.

Here are ten moments you can look forward to at this **self-catering game lodge in Mpumalanga**—each one unique to Jacana.

## 1. Waking Up to Mist Rising Off the Crocodile River

Two of Jacana’s five en-suite bedrooms face the river directly, creating slow, peaceful mornings where you open the curtains to mist drifting across the water and animals beginning their day.

## 2. Watching Elephants Walk Past as You Drink Your Morning Coffee

Mjejane Game Reserve is known for exceptional riverfront wildlife sightings. From Jacana’s wrap-around deck, it’s common to see elephants, hippos, antelope and more—no vehicle required.

## 3. Having the Entire Lodge to Yourself

Jacana is booked **exclusively to one group**. No strangers. No shared areas. Just your family or friends enjoying a private luxury space in the bush.

## 4. Kids Splashing in the Pool While Wildlife Moves Below

The rim-flow swimming pool overlooks the river, giving families a safe, relaxing, and unforgettable place to cool off between game viewing.

## 5. Long, Slow Braai Nights in the Boma

With a built-in braai and firepit, evenings at Jacana become firelit gatherings under open skies—perfect for stories, laughter, and stargazing.

## 6. Cooking Together in a Fully Equipped Kitchen

As a **luxury self-catering lodge**, Jacana gives groups the freedom to prepare meals their way, without fixed menus or dining schedules.

## 7. Gentle Afternoons on the River Deck

The elevated deck wraps around the lodge, offering shaded areas for reading, resting, or simply listening to the bush at its quietest.

## 8. Children Being Able to Explore Freely

With spacious indoor and outdoor areas, families can spread out comfortably, allowing children to play, swim, and relax in a secure private environment.

## 9. Movie Nights or Board Games in the Lounge

The double-volume lounge and TV room offer warm evening spaces for indoor downtime after a long day of river watching.

## 10. Feeling Completely Removed from the World

Mjejane’s tranquillity is unmatched. Once inside the reserve, everyday noise disappears, replaced by bird calls, distant lions, and the river flowing below.

**Jacana River Lodge** isn’t just another option among *game lodges in Mpumalanga*. It’s a place where small, powerful memories are made—moments that stay with you long after the trip ends.
