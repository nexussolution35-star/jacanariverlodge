# A Touch of Africa

Tailor-made bliss in the comfort of Jacana River Lodge. The mobile spa therapists will come to you.![Mjejane-Mobile-Bush-Spa](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Mjejane-Mobile-Bush-Spa.jpg)

You can enjoy a relaxed, unwinding treatment while the kids are playing in our courtyard. Or in the entertainment area, away from the balcony, overlooking the crocodile riverbank and the Greater Kruger National Park.

A safari holiday is a sensory experience so, with an abundance of exotic, yet earthy sights, sounds, tastes and smells. It’s certainly all about relaxing, deep breathing and slowing down.

Although the landscape offers a huge bounty of memorable moments, few experiences beat the sheer indulgence of a tailor-made massage in the privacy of your suite.

Mjejane Mobile Bush Spa not only comes to you with offers of pampering, restorative massages and treatments. But also deeply rooted in African tradition and themes, and using the finest locally sourced products.

The Collective Spirit of Africa is an absolute soul surrendering full body massage. Because of the use of a blend of African oils to ease away tension. A Chungu massage (Swahili for ‘strong’) is a deep tissue and pressure point massage, so to leave muscles energised and rivitalised.

### Good to know

You could choose a Hot Stone Journey, an African Hand or Footprint Ritual. Or the Essence of Africa Aromatherapy or Shamans Healing Reflexology, each treatment unique in the benefits it offers. Whichever indulgent treat you opt for, it is guaranteed to enrich your holiday, and grounded on the mother continent.

Booking beforehand is essential and subject to availability. Please inform your consultant of any underlying health issues when making the reservation, such as high blood pressure, allergies or pregnancies.
