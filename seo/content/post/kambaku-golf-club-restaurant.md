# The Kambaku Golf Club & Restaurant

Tee off with the stunning Lebombo Mountains as a backdrop at Kambaku Golf Club. Situated close to the Kruger National Park, our 9-hole course will challenge the skill level of any Golfer. Kambaku Golf Club also features a well-stocked bar with a breezy restaurant, the perfect relaxation spot after an exciting golfing experience.
