# Top Spot to Send Off the year with an African Safari

![Jacana River Lodge Logo with 5 star grading](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Jacana-Logo-2-5-Star-1024x836.png)

As the sun is setting on our year, it’s time to take a look at your bucket list ─ did you get to explore any of your dream destinations? If not, don’t fear, you’ve got enough time left to make a dent in that list! But first we have to wish the past year farewell, and what better way to do that than by admiring a spectacular sunset with your loved ones on an **african safari**? [Jacana River Lodge](https://jacanariverlodge.com) is the place to be!

#### Sunset from the veranda

![african safari with south african style braai](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/IMG_7950-1024x683.jpg)

Braving the Lowveld Kruger National Park heat is definitely worth the sweat since the sunsets here are truly breathtaking! The Crocodile River provide an extra warm reflection on the water as the setting sun serves as background and canvas to leopard trees, thorny bush and grazing Impala and black rhino.

Combine your Kruger National Park New Year’s celebration with game viewing in our own Jacana River Lodge game viewer where you can take delight in our abundant variety of the big five wildlife.

[Book now!](https://www.travelground.com/accommodation/jacana-river-lodge)

There is also nothing like the bushveld to bid the year farewell. With lions roaring in the distance. Certainly preparing for a night of hunting. The enchantment of the Mjejane private game reserve attracts loads of wildlife from the north. And they settle right on the banks of the crocodile river almost at your doorstep. All you have to do to experience the magic of the mjejane lifestyle yourself, is pack your bags and visit Jacana River Lodge with your family and friends. With Jacana Lodge being child friendly, an African Safari for the family and your friends is [now available](https://jacanariverlodge.com/book-now/).

You can enjoy every sunset right from Jacana River Lodge balcony, drinking your favourite sundowner at the bar, or you can go sight seeing on a real true african safari at the nearest water hole. The Kruger sunsets are incomparable! Certainly worth more than a million dollars… It just doesn’t get any better than this!

[Secure your spot](https://www.travelground.com/accommodation/jacana-river-lodge) before it is too late.

![self catering luxury lodge africa safari](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/IMG_7980-1024x683.jpg)

At night the rumble of the elephants instills a peace of mind that you will only find in Africa. And so, my soul has been replenished and restored for a new year. Now I can take 2020 by the horns, with new vision and focus.
