# Get up close and personal with Africa's Giants

.

Low Hills Safaris offers an educational experience in the bush with the Kwa Madwala bull elephant and his handler of 18 years who is also a senior field guide, Sonell.

No rides or tricks, simply experience the majesty of this animal in quiet nature.
