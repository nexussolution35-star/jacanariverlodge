# A Family Safari Adventure at Jacana River Lodge – Exploring the Mjejane Game Reserve

![lion pride of mjejane game reserve (1)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/lion-pride-of-mjejane-game-reserve-1.png)

Imagine waking up to the gentle sounds of the African bush—a lion's distant roar, the chatter of birds greeting the sunrise, and the rustling of leaves as an elephant grazes nearby. This is the daily reality at Jacana River Lodge, a luxurious self-catering retreat for families and large groups located within the spectacular [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/).

Join us as we follow one family’s safari adventure and discover why Jacana River Lodge is the perfect destination for your next group getaway.

## Day 1: Arrival at Jacana River Lodge

The Smith family arrives at Jacana River Lodge in the early afternoon. Each member of the family is thrilled with their room—complete with en-suite bathrooms and views of the Crocodile River. The children race out to the viewing deck, where they spot a herd of impalas grazing by the riverbank.

The rest of the afternoon is spent unpacking, taking a refreshing dip in the infinity pool, and firing up the braai for a relaxed family dinner under the stars. As night falls, the bush comes alive with nocturnal sounds. The family gathers around the deck, sharing stories as they listen to the distant call of a hyena.

## Day 2: Guided Game Drive in the Mjejane Game Reserve

The next morning, the family wakes before dawn to prepare for their first guided game drive. Their ranger, James, arrives promptly with the safari vehicle, ready to take them deep into [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/).

The early morning light paints the landscape in warm hues as they set off. Within minutes, they encounter their first major sighting—a pride of lions lounging near a waterhole. James explains the social dynamics of the pride, captivating both the kids and adults with his knowledge.

As the drive continues, they spot elephants, giraffes, and a dazzle of zebras. The children eagerly snap photos while their parents marvel at the sheer diversity of wildlife. James shares tips on tracking animals and identifying bird calls, making the experience both educational and entertaining.

The highlight of the drive comes when they witness a herd of buffalo crossing the road, kicking up clouds of dust as they move in unison.

## Day 3: Exploring the Reserve on a Self-Drive Safari in the Kruger Park

Having gained confidence from their guided experience, the Smiths decide to embark on a self-drive safari in the Kruger Park. Armed with a map of the reserve and plenty of snacks, they set out to explore at their own pace.

They take a route along the Crocodile River, stopping at scenic viewpoints to observe hippos basking in the water and crocodiles sunning themselves on the banks. The kids are excited to spot a family of warthogs darting across the road, their tails raised like flags.

With no strict schedule to follow, the family enjoys the freedom to linger at sightings and take in the beauty of the landscape. They even encounter a large herd of elephants, including a playful calf that entertains them with its clumsy attempts to drink from the river.

## Day 4: A Day of Relaxation and Wildlife Viewing

After two days of adventure, the family decides to spend a relaxing day at the lodge. The morning is spent birdwatching from the deck, where they spot kingfishers, storks, and a pair of fish eagles soaring above the river.

Later, the children take another swim in the pool while their parents enjoy a peaceful afternoon nap. Wildlife continues to pass by the lodge, offering a front-row seat to nature’s theater.

In the evening, they gather around the outdoor fire pit for a traditional bush dinner. As the sun sets, the sky transforms into a canvas of stars. The family marvels at the clarity of the night sky, spotting constellations they rarely see back home.

## Day 5: Farewell to the Bush

On their final morning, the family takes one last stroll around the lodge grounds. They reflect on the unforgettable moments they’ve shared—witnessing the Big Five, learning about the ecosystem, and spending quality time together away from the distractions of daily life.

With bags packed and hearts full, they say goodbye to Jacana River Lodge, promising to return for another safari adventure.
