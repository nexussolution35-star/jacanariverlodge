# Educational Wildlife Escapes: Discovering Nature with Kids at Jacana River Lodge near Kruger National Park

![](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/DSC_6546-scaled.jpg)

Embark on a journey of discovery with your family at [Jacana River Lodge](../), nestled within the pristine wilderness of the [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/), bordering the iconic Kruger National Park. Our lodge offers the perfect setting for families to connect with nature and learn about the wonders of the African bush.

As you step onto the veranda, deck, and pool area, you'll be greeted by breathtaking views of the river, providing the perfect backdrop for outdoor exploration and relaxation. Take advantage of the ice machine and service counter, or cool off in our rim-flow swimming pool while enjoying the sights and sounds of the surrounding wilderness.

For a taste of authentic African cuisine, head to the boma or one of the covered braai areas, where you can prepare delicious meals using the provided cooking tools and firewood. The upper deck and bar offer a picturesque setting for watching African sunsets and hosting memorable gatherings with your loved ones.

At Jacana River Lodge, every detail is designed with families in mind. Our lodge facilities include a scullery with washing and dishwashing machines, as well as secure parking for your vehicles. Families can relax knowing that our courtyard is a safe haven for children to play, with a well-maintained lawn and jungle gym providing hours of entertainment.

Inside, our kiddies playroom and small gym offer a variety of educational activities, from watching wildlife documentaries to engaging in interactive games and exercises. Each of our five en-suite bedrooms provides a comfortable and welcoming retreat, with luxurious amenities ensuring a restful night's sleep for the whole family.

Join us at Jacana River Lodge for an educational wildlife escape, where learning and adventure go hand in hand. From guided bush walks to wildlife tracking experiences, every moment is an opportunity to discover the beauty and diversity of the African bush.
