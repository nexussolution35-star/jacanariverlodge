# Rhino Rendezvous at Jacana River Lodge: A Self-Catering Escape in the Heart of Mjejane

Welcome to Jacana River Lodge, your sanctuary in the South African bushveld, where the untamed spirit of the [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/) meets the comfort of a home away from home. Nestled on the banks of the Crocodile River, Jacana beckons nature lovers and families seeking a tranquil retreat amidst the wonders of Mpumalanga.

## Jacana River Lodge: Your Bushveld Home

Jacana River Lodge stands as a testament to the beauty of the bushveld, offering a unique blend of self-catering comfort and thrilling game viewing. Our lodge boasts five en-suite bedrooms, an expansive kitchen, a cozy lounge, and a spacious dining area, providing the perfect setting for family holidays. With a child-friendly environment, Jacana ensures a memorable escape for the entire family.

## Amenities for the Soul

Our lodge embraces the essence of relaxation with a host of amenities, including a sprawling infinity pool overlooking the game reserve, a large braai area for communal feasts, secure parking, and complimentary Wi-Fi access. As a 5-star self-catering establishment, Jacana River Lodge is not just a destination; it's a haven for a leisurely kuier or a rejuvenating family holiday.

## Mjejane Private Nature Reserve: A Slice of Paradise

Mjejane Private Nature Reserve, situated on the southern boundary of the Kruger National Park, is a haven for wildlife enthusiasts. The reserve, part of the Greater Kruger, is renowned for its exclusive charm and the gentle flow of the Crocodile River. Jacana River Lodge stands proudly among the game lodges along the riverbanks, offering guests an unparalleled opportunity to witness the beauty of the African bush.

## Wildlife Encounters on Your Doorstep

The Mjejane Private Nature Reserve, with its lush riverine forest, unfolds a vibrant tapestry of bird and animal life. Jacana invites you to experience the magic of early morning and late evening safaris, where leopards, elephants, and the grunting sounds of hippo pods paint an authentic African panorama. The Crocodile River becomes a theater of nature's drama, featuring a captivating cast of plains game, predators, and an orchestra of birdlife.

## Rhino Odyssey: Conservation and Connection

At Jacana, our commitment extends beyond providing a luxurious escape. We are proud custodians of rhino conservation, offering guests the chance to witness these magnificent creatures in their natural habitat. The Mjejane is home to both white and black rhinos, adding a touch of wonder to your safari experience.
