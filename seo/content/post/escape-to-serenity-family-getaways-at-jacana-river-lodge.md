# Creating Cherished Moments: Family Getaways at Jacana River Lodge near Kruger National Park

![Jacana-River-Lodge-Wildlife-9](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Jacana-River-Lodge-Wildlife-9.jpg)

Escape the ordinary and embark on a family getaway like no other at [Jacana River Lodge](../), situated in the heart of the [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/), just moments away from the world-renowned Kruger National Park. With its tranquil surroundings and family-friendly amenities, Jacana River Lodge promises an unforgettable retreat for all ages.

Indulge in ultimate relaxation on the veranda, deck, and pool area, where panoramic views of the river and surrounding wilderness provide the perfect backdrop for quality family time. Whether you're dining alfresco, lounging in the sun, or taking a refreshing dip in the pool, every moment is infused with serenity and bliss.

As the sun sets, gather around the boma or one of the covered braai areas to savor delicious meals prepared on the open flame, while the upper deck and bar offer a prime vantage point for watching the African sky transform into a canvas of vibrant colors.

Our lodge facilities cater to every need, with additional amenities including a scullery equipped with washing and dishwashing machines, extra fridges, and secure parking for your vehicles. Families can relax knowing that our courtyard is a safe haven for children to play, with a well-maintained lawn and jungle gym providing hours of entertainment.

Inside, our kiddies playroom and small gym offer a variety of activities to keep young ones occupied, from watching TV to playing games and exercising. Each of our five en-suite bedrooms provides a comfortable and welcoming retreat, with luxurious amenities ensuring a restful night's sleep for the whole family.

Join us at Jacana River Lodge for a family getaway filled with cherished moments and unforgettable adventures, where the magic of the African wilderness awaits.
