![nicole-wreyford-rhino mjejane game reserve jacana river lodge](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/nicole-wreyford-rhino-mjejane-game-reserve-jacana-river-lodge.jpg)

Mpumalanga is often synonymous with the Kruger National Park, but just beyond Kruger’s southern boundary lies a lesser-known treasure: [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/). This privately managed reserve offers visitors the chance to experience South Africa’s legendary wildlife and landscapes with added exclusivity and tranquility. For travelers looking to immerse themselves in nature without the crowds, Mjejane is a top choice.

In this post, we’ll explore what makes Mjejane Game Reserve special and why Jacana River Lodge is the perfect base for your adventure.

## Where Is Mjejane Game Reserve?

Located on the southern border of the Kruger National Park, Mjejane Game Reserve is easily accessible from major towns like Nelspruit and Malelane. The reserve stretches over 10,000 hectares along the Crocodile River and forms part of the greater Kruger ecosystem. Wildlife moves freely between Mjejane and Kruger, offering visitors the chance to spot the Big Five and other iconic animals in a pristine environment.

## What Makes Mjejane Game Reserve Unique?

Here are some key features that set Mjejane apart from other game reserves in Mpumalanga:

### Unfenced Border with Kruger

Unlike many private reserves, Mjejane shares an unfenced border with Kruger National Park, allowing for the natural movement of wildlife between the two areas.

### Exclusive Access

Since Mjejane is privately managed, visitor numbers are limited, ensuring a more intimate safari experience.

### Diverse Habitats

The reserve boasts a variety of ecosystems, including riverine forests, savannahs, and rocky outcrops. This diversity attracts a wide range of animals and birds.

### Family-Friendly Environment

Mjejane offers a safe, relaxed atmosphere that’s perfect for families. Self-drive routes and private lodges like Jacana River Lodge provide flexibility and comfort for all ages.

## Wildlife Highlights in Mjejane Game Reserve

Mjejane is a haven for wildlife enthusiasts. Whether you’re a first-time visitor or a seasoned safari-goer, the reserve offers unforgettable sightings:

* **Big Five Sightings:** Lions, leopards, rhinos, elephants, and buffaloes are frequently spotted within the reserve.
* **Birdwatching Paradise:** Over 300 bird species have been recorded in Mjejane, including African fish eagles, hornbills, and lilac-breasted rollers.
* **Aquatic Life:** The Crocodile River attracts hippos, crocodiles, and a variety of waterbirds.

## The Best Time to Visit Mjejane Game Reserve

The reserve is open year-round, but each season offers a different experience:

* **Dry Season (May to October):** This is the best time for wildlife viewing, as animals congregate around water sources. Cooler temperatures make game drives more comfortable.
* **Green Season (November to April):** The reserve transforms into a lush, green paradise with dramatic thunderstorms and plenty of newborn animals. Birdwatchers will also enjoy the arrival of migratory species.

## Activities to Enjoy in and Around Mjejane Game Reserve

Visitors to Mjejane can enjoy a wide range of activities, both within the reserve and in the surrounding region:

* **Game Drives:** Explore the reserve on professionally guided game drives, or embark on a self-drive safari for a more personal experience.
