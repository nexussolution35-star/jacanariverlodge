# THE KINGSLAND RECREATIONAL FACILITY

The Kingsland picnic facility is one of Mjejane’s hidden gems. Perfectly situated with lots of birdlife and is the perfect place to unwind and relax under the Tamboti trees.

On Kingsland you can explore the walking trails, dare to take on the cycling routes and enjoy a relaxing bush braai that accommodates 12 people.

![Kingsland Recreational Area Mjejane Game Reserve](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Kingsland-Recreational-Area-Mjejane-Game-Reserve.png)

## Booking Procedure:

1. Bookings are essential for bush braai’s and can be booked with Melissa Gloy via e-mail (hoa@mjejane.com) 48 hours in advance.

2. Melissa Gloy from Mjejane HOA will confirm availability of the facility.
