# Game Drives in the Kruger National Park and Mjejane Private Game Reserve

#### At Jacana River Lodge you have 3 Game viewing options available:

Wildlife, including the Big 5, roam freely between the KNP and Mjejane Private Game Reserve. Jacana River Lodge has its own private, exclusive game viewing vehicle which you can hire for a full or half-day to enjoy the +- 4000 hectares of the Mjejane Private Game Reserve.

Hop in a 10-seater KNP game viewing vehicle and explore the raw African Bushveld and experience the most incredible wildlife encounters. Book your exciting African Safari directly with the Kruger National Park – [here](http://www.sanparks.org/)

**Game Drives in Kruger Park** offers you the opportunity to encounter the Big 5. You can experience the thrill of your first night drive, and learn more about the small creatures from your field guide. You can arrange any Game Drives in the Kruger National Park through Mjejane Private Game Reserve field guides and rangers when you stay at Jacana River Lodge.

Tourists visit Kruger Park to view the big game, in particular the big 5. Buffalo, elephant, lion, leopard, and rhinoceros classifies as the Big 5. There are, however, much more [wildlife](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wildlife-and-sightings/)species to see. One of the best ways to experience the diversity of the Park is through a game drive with one of our trained guides at [Mjejane Game Reserve](https://jacanariverlodge.com/2020/10/01/live-the-dream-at-mjejane-lifestyle-game-reserve/).

Kruger Park uses vehicles of 10-seats, as well as the more economical 20-seater trucks. **Jacana River Lodge** has their own private 10 seater game viewer that you can book in advance. For private family game drives, Jacana River Lodge caters to the whole family. The rangers from Mjejane Game Reserve are always available to take you and your family on an exciting tour through the Kruger National Park. Kruger Park has 2 types of vehicles. Availability will vary amongst the Kruger Park Restcamps and it is not possible to book the type of vehicle in advance. When [booking](https://jacanariverlodge.com/book-now/)[accommodation](https://jacanariverlodge.com/2020/08/01/5-star-self-catering-accommodation/), please check.

### Kruger Park offers the following public game drives:

###### **\* Sunrise drives**

Kruger Park offers sunrise drives at the following camps: Bateleur Bushcamp, Berg en Dal Restcamp, Letaba Restcamp, Lower Sabie Restcamp, Olifants Restcamp, Orpen Restcamp, Pretoriuskop Restcamp, Punda Maria Restcamp, Satara Restcamp, Shimuwini Bushveld Camp, Shingwedzi Restcamp, Skukuza Restcamp, and Crocodile Bridge Restcamp.

The sunrise drive leaves at around 04.30 in summer and 05.30 in winter. Clients are to confirm with reception the previous night when they book into their accommodation.

###### **\* Sunset drives**

Sunset drives are offered at the following camps: Bateleur Bushcamp, Berg en Dal Restcamp, Biyamiti Bush Camp, Crocodile Bridge Restcamp, Letaba Restcamp, Lower Sabie Restcamp, Mopani Restcamp, Olifants Restcamp, Orpen Restcamp, Pretoriuskop Restcamp, Punda Maria Restcamp, Satara Restcamp, Shimuwini Bushveld Camp, Shingwedzi Restcamp, Skukuza Restcamp and Talamati Bushveld Camp.

You can also take part in the nocturnal activities of Kruger’s secretive creatures. Experienced guides drive the groups. The drives duration is also 3-4 hours leaving the camp at about 16.00

Jacana River Lodge offers private game drives for the whole family. You can book your own ranger with us. Just click on the WhatsApp window below in the corner or [book your accommodation with Jacana River Lodge](https://jacanariverlodge.com/book-now/)

###### \* Night drives

Night drives are offered at the following restcamps: Bateleur Bushcamp, Crocodile Bridge Restcamp, Letaba Restcamp, Lower Sabie Restcamp, Mopani Restcamp, Olifants Restcamp, Punda Maria Restcamp, Satara Restcamp, Shimuwini Bushveld Camp, Shingwedzi Restcamp, Skukuza Restcamp and Talamati Bushveld Camp.

### **Booking of game drives in the Kruger National Park**

You can book your Kruger National Park Game Drive online at <http://www.sanparks.org/> website or Email them at [reservations@sanparks.org](mailto:reservations@sanparks.org) or telephonically on 012 428 9111.
