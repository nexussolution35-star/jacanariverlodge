This format is especially appealing for families and multi-generational groups. ## The Jacana River Lodge Experience — Luxury in Every Detail

Jacana River Lodge accommodates up to ten guests in five beautifully appointed en-suite bedrooms. Two of these rooms overlook the Crocodile River, providing breathtaking sunrise views and daily wildlife encounters. The remaining rooms offer privacy and comfort, each designed with families and groups in mind. At the heart of the lodge is a double-volume lounge that opens directly onto a wrap-around viewing deck. The boma and built-in braai complete the experience, turning every evening into a fireside celebration under the stars.

Unlike many **game lodges in Mpumalanga** that cater to multiple groups at once, Jacana is *exclusive-use*—booked to one party at a time. That means no shared spaces, no competing schedules, and no outside noise. It’s just you, your family, and the natural soundtrack of the bush.

## Why Mpumalanga and Mjejane Are the Ultimate Safari Destinations

Located in South Africa’s scenic Lowveld, Mpumalanga is home to some of the most sought-after private game reserves in the country. **Mjejane Game Reserve**, where Jacana River Lodge is situated, is particularly special—it shares an unfenced boundary with the world-famous Kruger National Park, allowing animals to roam freely between the two. Guests can witness Big Five sightings without ever leaving the property.

The reserve’s riverfront position offers unmatched game-viewing opportunities year-round. In the dry season, animals gather at the water’s edge; in summer, lush greenery and birdlife transform the scenery into a photographer’s dream. This rare blend of tranquillity and excitement makes **Mjejane accommodation** like Jacana highly sought after for both domestic and international travellers.

Another key advantage is accessibility. Mjejane lies within easy driving distance of Malelane Gate, the southern entrance to Kruger National Park, as well as nearby towns offering supplies, fuel, and grocery options. That makes Jacana an ideal base for longer stays—it’s remote enough to feel wild, but close enough to remain convenient.
