###### 5 Reasons Families Love Self-Catering at Jacana River Lodge in May

## 5 Reasons Families Love Self-Catering at Jacana River Lodge in May

May is one of the most popular months for families choosing a private self-catering safari in Mjejane Game Reserve. The pleasant autumn weather, combined with the complete freedom of a 5-star exclusive lodge, makes Jacana River Lodge the perfect choice for multi-generational family holidays. Here are five big reasons why families are loving self-catering at Jacana this May.

### 1. Complete Control Over Your Family’s Schedule

No more 5 am wake-up calls or fixed meal times. In May you can sleep in, enjoy a leisurely breakfast on the veranda while watching the Crocodile River, and decide each day whether to do a game drive or simply relax. This flexibility is especially appreciated by families with young children and grandparents who prefer a gentler pace.

### 2. Your Own Private Infinity Pool with River Views

May’s warm days (26–30°C) are perfect for pool time. Jacana’s spacious rim-flow infinity pool overlooks the Crocodile River, where elephants, hippos and buffalo regularly come to drink. Families spend lazy afternoons swimming and game viewing from the water — all in complete privacy because the lodge is booked for your group only.

### 3. Child-Friendly Facilities That Keep Everyone Happy

The dedicated kiddies playroom (with TV, games, pool table and mini gym) and outdoor jungle gym with swings and slide give children a safe space to burn energy. Parents can relax on the deck or in the lounge knowing the kids are happily occupied and secure. This thoughtful design makes May family holidays stress-free and enjoyable for all ages.

### 4. Fully Equipped Kitchen + Domestic Staff Support

Self-catering at Jacana means real freedom in the kitchen. The sprawling kitchen includes a double-door fridge, gas stove, oven, Nespresso machine, dishwasher and everything you need. Full-time domestic staff handle cleaning (weekdays and Saturday mornings), so you get the joy of cooking family meals and braais without the chores. Perfect for picky eaters and special diets.

### 5. Magical Evenings in Your Private Boma

Cool May evenings are ideal for traditional South African braais. Gather around the covered boma or large braai areas (firewood supplied daily) under the stars while listening to the sounds of the bush. The upper-level bar and deck are perfect for family sundowners before dinner. These relaxed evenings create the special memories that families talk about for years.

With its exclusive-use policy, riverfront location, and thoughtful family features, Jacana River Lodge delivers the perfect self-catering experience in May. You get luxury, privacy, flexibility and excellent wildlife viewing — all at your own pace.
