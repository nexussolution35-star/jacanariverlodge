# Mjejane Lifestyle Game Reserve: Live the dream

Where the Crocodile River marks the Kruger National Park border, an unparalleled lifestyle opportunity awaits the discerning African Safari tourist. Mjejane Lifestyle comprises two dream escapes: Riverview and Bushlands.

![Pride of lions in Mjejane Lifestyle Game reserve](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Pride-of-lions-in-Mjejane-Lifestyle-Game-reserve.jpg)

In a world where true escapes and authentic experiences are getting scarcer, Mjejane Lifestyle offers something unique. Set in the indigenous bush environment of the greater 4 000 ha Mjejane Game Reserve. On the southern boundary of the Kruger National Park, the reserve enjoys 10 kilometers of river frontage. The reserve is also fenced into the KNP.

Here, in this pristine piece of undisturbed Africa, a limited number of riverfront and bushland accommodation are available. Riverview stands overlook the Crocodile River and its steady stream of meandering wildlife. On the other hand, Bushland units are set in denser vegetation. So, you have more intimate Big 5 experiences. This is the absolute opportunity to enjoy an African Safari within the magical Mjejane Game Reserve and all its abundance.

![Mjejane Lifestyle Game Reserve Riverview area](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/IMG_7927-1024x683.jpg)

The reserve’s sustainable model also became an international benchmark for tourism. Especially through community involvement. Thus through research, careful planning and selective development, the owners of Mjejane Lifestyle Estate secured this natural resource. Firstly for conservation. And secondly, successfully maintained and improved this natural resource.

And although the world is currently in turmoil, Mjejane has normal accessibility from every major South African centre. So this makes it particularly attractive as a Safari destination. Situated a mere four hours’ drive from Gauteng. Or, 45 minutes from Kruger Mpumalanga International Airport and 90 minutes from Maputo. It is just around the corner on the one hand, but a universe of worry and stress away on the other. [Book your holiday](https://jacanariverlodge.com/book-now/) in this unspoilt part of Africa.

###### **Mjejane Riverview**

The Mjejane Lifestyle Riverview area, Kingsland, invite only a fortunate few to enjoy blissful vistas over the Crocodile River and into the Kruger Park. Here, visitors will watch nature play out before them from the comfort and privacy of their own exclusive deck.

###### **Mjejane Bushland**

![Mjejane Bushland](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Mjejane-Bushland.jpg)

The Bushland are for the real bush lovers. So, when you are looking for a more intimate place of retreat, Jacana River Lodge is the perfect getaway. Defined by the natural beauty, undisturbed tranquility and privacy, it allows you to get up close and personal with the Big 5. Alongside the seasonal streambeds and flanked by riverine trees. A fence, with low Kenyan-style electric fences, keep the big game within view without posing a threat to humans.
