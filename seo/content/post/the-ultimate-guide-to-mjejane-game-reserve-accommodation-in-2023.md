### Why is a private game reserve, like Mjejane, the better option?

2023 is set to be the year of travelling. And what better way to start the year than by going into the bush and settling into that slow and peaceful vibe that you can only get when in the wild?

When planning your bushveld trip, you have two options. You could head into the more conventional wildlife parks, like the Kruger which is open to the public, or you can instead opt for private game reserve like the Mjejane, which is more secluded.

Aside from the accommodation being a lot different when you are staying in a private park at a luxury lodge like Jacana River Lodge, there are a few other ways that opting for a private game reserve in 2023 might be the better choice for you.

1. No tourist crowds. One of the greatest things about staying in a private game reserve like the Mjejane, is that there are no crowds of people on the roads or in shared spaces. Given what has happened during the past 2 years, wanting more secluded or uncrowded spaces is completely natural and something that some people might actively look for in their holiday retreat.

2. The best rangers and guides. Some of the most knowledgeable game guides are based in the private game reserves, and sometimes they are attached to specific game lodges.

3. Longer wildlife viewings. In those more crowded game reserves, the general unwritten rule is that you should only spend a little bit of time at each wildlife sighting, to give everyone else the opportunity to get a look. But with fewer people in a private game park, you can enjoy the sighting for longer.
