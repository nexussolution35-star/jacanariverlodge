## 10 Self-Catering Lodge Features You’ll Love at Jacana River Lodge

You decide when to wake up, when to eat, and how to spend your days. With five en-suite bedrooms, a rim-flow pool, and views of the Crocodile River, the lodge is perfect for **family getaways, group holidays, and private bush retreats**.

Here are ten features that make Jacana River Lodge one of the best **self-catering safari lodges in Mpumalanga**—and why guests keep coming back year after year.

## 1. A Fully Equipped Kitchen That Feels Like Home

At Jacana, self-catering doesn’t mean sacrificing comfort. The kitchen is modern, spacious, and equipped with everything your group could need: a double fridge/freezer, gas stove, electric oven, microwave, scullery, and ample counter space. Whether you’re preparing a quick breakfast before a game drive or a slow-cooked dinner after sunset, it’s designed for easy, group-style cooking. The open-plan layout connects seamlessly with the indoor dining and lounge area, keeping everyone together while meals are prepared.

Unlike many lodges that limit guests to basic amenities, Jacana’s kitchen invites you to make it your own—perfect for food lovers, families with dietary needs, or anyone who enjoys the ritual of cooking together. It’s one of the most liberating features of a true **self-catering safari lodge**.

## 2. Private Boma and Braai Area for Fireside Evenings

No South African safari is complete without a night under the stars, and Jacana’s outdoor entertainment area delivers the ultimate experience. The built-in braai and traditional boma let guests enjoy authentic bush dining without leaving the property. Picture crackling wood, the glow of firelight, and the sound of hyenas in the distance—it’s where the day slows down and stories come alive.

Because the lodge is booked for your group alone, you can linger by the fire as long as you like, free from interruptions. Whether it’s a family braai or a quiet night of stargazing, the boma becomes the heart of your stay—private, peaceful, and deeply memorable.

One of Jacana’s most celebrated features is its expansive wrap-around viewing deck overlooking the Crocodile River. It’s your front-row seat to the wild, where elephants, buffalo, antelope, and even lions can be spotted without setting foot in a vehicle.
