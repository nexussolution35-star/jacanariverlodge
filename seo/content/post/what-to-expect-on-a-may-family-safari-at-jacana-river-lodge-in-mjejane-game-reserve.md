## What to Expect on a May Family Safari at Jacana River Lodge in Mjejane Game Reserve

Planning a May 2026 family safari? Here’s exactly what you can expect at Jacana River Lodge in Mjejane Game Reserve — one of the most comfortable and enjoyable months for a private self-catering holiday near Kruger National Park.

### 1. Pleasant Autumn Weather

May brings warm, sunny days (26–30°C) and cool, comfortable evenings. It’s the perfect balance — warm enough for swimming in the infinity pool during the day, yet cool enough for cosy boma fires at night. No more summer humidity or heavy rains.

### 2. Relaxed Daily Rhythm

Because the lodge is exclusively yours, you set the pace. Sleep in if you want. Enjoy coffee on the veranda while watching the Crocodile River. Decide each morning whether to book a private game drive or simply relax and enjoy lodge-based wildlife viewing. This flexibility is ideal for families with different ages and energy levels.

### 3. Wildlife Right on Your Doorstep

May is excellent for game viewing. As the bush thins, animals are easier to spot. From the infinity pool, deck or veranda you’ll often see elephant, hippo, buffalo and sometimes the Big 5 moving along the river. Birdlife is also very active with fish eagles, purple-crested louries and many others.

### 4. Family-Friendly Facilities in Full Use

The kiddies playroom and jungle gym get plenty of use in May’s pleasant weather. Kids can play safely while parents unwind. The spacious 5-bedroom layout, open-plan lounge, and wheelchair-friendly room make it easy for multi-generational groups to stay comfortable and connected.

### 5. Magical Evenings Around the Boma

Cool May evenings are perfect for traditional braais. Gather in the covered boma with firewood supplied daily, enjoy sundowners from the upper-level bar, and listen to the sounds of the bush at night. These relaxed family moments are what guests remember most.

A May stay at Jacana River Lodge delivers the best of both worlds: excellent wildlife viewing, comfortable weather, complete privacy, and the freedom of true 5-star self-catering. It’s a relaxed, authentic safari experience that works beautifully for families.
