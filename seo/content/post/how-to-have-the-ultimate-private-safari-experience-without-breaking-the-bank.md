###### How to Have the Ultimate Private Safari Experience (Without Breaking the Bank)

![](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Jacana-River-Lodge-Wildlife-35-720x480.jpg)![Homepage Banner - Game Lodges in Mpumalanga - Jacana Lodge](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Jacana-River-Game-Lodge-Home-Banner-1jpg.jpg)

## Why Private Safaris Are the New Standard for Group Travel

For many travelers, the dream of a private safari conjures up images of luxury, exclusivity, and hefty price tags—but that’s changing fast. As more families, friend groups, and small collectives seek meaningful time together in nature, private-use safari lodges like Jacana River Lodge are leading a new trend: high-end safari experiences designed to be affordable when shared.

Unlike traditional safari lodges, where you might share meals, game drives, and living spaces with strangers, a private safari lodge offers full control of your schedule, meals, and accommodation. There’s no set mealtime, no rushing for the game vehicle, and no competing for views on the deck. Your group books the entire property—and everything is tailored to your pace, your needs, and your vision of the perfect bush getaway.

Jacana River Lodge, located in Mjejane Game Reserve on the Crocodile River’s edge, offers one of the best examples of this trend. The lodge accommodates up to 10 guests across five en-suite bedrooms, and is booked as a full unit. That means you get the entire lodge—riverfront deck, rim-flow pool, boma, braai area, and luxury lounge—all to yourself. And because it’s self-catering, you skip the cost of daily meals and staff, without compromising on comfort.

This model is ideal for groups looking to split costs without sacrificing experience. When the lodge rate is divided among 6–10 guests, the per-person rate can rival (or beat) the cost of a mid-range hotel—yet what you receive is miles ahead in terms of luxury, location, and privacy.

What’s more, private lodges offer a level of flexibility that traditional safaris don’t. Want to sleep in and skip the morning drive? No problem. Prefer to cook your own food and enjoy lazy lunches on the deck? Go for it. Jacana lets you set your own rhythm, which is especially appealing for multi-generational families, groups with children, or friends wanting quality time without outside schedules interfering.

Another reason private-use lodges are gaining traction is the post-COVID shift toward secure, contained travel. With no other guests or shared facilities, you dramatically reduce contact with strangers—without sacrificing the experience. For many, it’s the best of both worlds: five-star surroundings and complete control over the environment.

Add to that the setting: Mjejane is a Big Five reserve with permeable boundaries to the Kruger National Park, meaning wildlife roams freely. From your private deck, it’s common to see elephants, hippos, buffalo, and even lions along the riverbank—making the safari come to you.

In short, the rise of private safari lodges isn’t just about exclusivity. It’s about value, intimacy, and the freedom to experience the bush your way. And when you find a lodge like Jacana River Lodge, where every corner is designed for relaxation, comfort, and wildlife connection, it becomes clear: the ultimate safari doesn’t need to come with a luxury hotel bill.

## Tips to Maximise Value at a Private Safari Lodge

A private safari might sound expensive—but with smart planning and the right lodge, you can enjoy an exclusive wildlife experience without stretching your budget. At Jacana River Lodge, value isn’t just about the price tag—it’s about how much more you get for what you pay.

### 1. Book as a Group and Split the Cost

Jacana River Lodge is a 5-bedroom luxury villa that sleeps up to 10 people. That means you can divide the total rate across multiple adults—often ending up with a per-person cost that’s lower than many catered lodges, even those with shared amenities. Whether you’re a family, a group of couples, or friends travelling together, this is one of the most cost-effective ways to access the luxury safari market.

### 2. Save on Meals with Self-Catering

Catered safari lodges charge a premium for meals, often regardless of whether you want a full breakfast or a light snack. Jacana’s self-catering model gives you full control over your food budget. Bring groceries from nearby Malelane or Komatipoort and prepare your own meals in the fully equipped kitchen. Braais under the stars, riverside breakfasts, and shared family dinners all come without extra cost—and they’re usually better than restaurant meals anyway.

### 3. Game Viewing Without the Drive

Another area where Jacana saves you money is transport. Many safari experiences require long drives into Kruger or expensive private game drives to see wildlife. At Jacana, much of the wildlife comes to you. With river frontage in a Big 5 reserve, you’ll often spot elephants, buffalo, hippos, and antelope without even leaving the pool deck. This passive game viewing can be just as fulfilling—and far more relaxed.

### 4. Bring Your Own Extras

Because you’re staying in a private space, you can bring your own snacks, drinks, and comforts without paying a premium. Stock up on your favourite wine or soft drinks, bring board games, Bluetooth speakers, or even birthday decorations if you’re celebrating. Jacana provides the setting—you personalise it however you like, at no extra cost.

### 5. Travel During Shoulder Seasons

Winter (June to August) is fantastic for game viewing, but May and September are often overlooked and come with lower rates. These months offer beautiful weather, fewer crowds, and great wildlife activity. Booking just outside peak periods can unlock excellent deals while still giving you a top-tier experience.

### 6. Make it a 3–5 Day Stay

Jacana’s true value unfolds over a few days. Unlike a one-night hotel stopover, this lodge invites you to settle in, unwind, and make the most of its facilities. A 3- to 5-day stay lets you stretch your cost per night, spend less on activities, and absorb more of the bush at your own pace—without the rush.

### In Conclusion

A private safari doesn’t need to be out of reach. By choosing a self-catering lodge like Jacana River Lodge, travelling in a group, and embracing flexibility, you unlock all the magic of a private safari—without breaking the bank. It’s not just about savings; it’s about better value, more freedom, and a deeper connection to the bush.
