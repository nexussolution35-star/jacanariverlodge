### 1. Location Matters: Why Families Choose Mjejane Game Reserve for School Holidays

Mjejane Game Reserve is one of South Africa’s most sought-after family safari destinations—and for good reason. Situated on the southern boundary of the Kruger National Park, the reserve offers:

* Big 5 wildlife
* riverfront game viewing
* exceptional safety
* easy accessibility from Johannesburg or Nelspruit
* controlled access points
* a quieter, more private safari experience than the public park

For families, this is ideal. Parents can relax knowing they’re in a secure, well-managed reserve with breathtaking natural scenery and consistent wildlife sightings.

**Mjejane’s biggest advantage?** Animals move freely between Kruger and the reserve thanks to its permeable boundaries. This means children can often see elephants, hippos, antelope, giraffe, and even predators—without leaving the lodge.

If your 2026 school holidays include the dream of waking up to elephants drinking from the river, Mjejane Game Reserve is where it becomes reality.
