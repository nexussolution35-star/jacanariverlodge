# Why Jacana River Lodge Is One of the Top-Rated Game Lodges in Mpumalanga

Nestled within the renowned Mjejane Game Reserve, adjacent to the Kruger National Park, Jacana River Lodge offers a luxurious, family-friendly safari experience. With permeable fences allowing free movement of wildlife between the reserves, guests are treated to an authentic bush adventure. This exclusive-use lodge has garnered exceptional reviews, making it a standout choice for those seeking premier accommodation near the Kruger National Park.

## Exceptional Guest Experiences

Guests consistently praise Jacana River Lodge for its impeccable location and amenities. One reviewer noted,

> “I loved everything about this house, the location was perfect as you get visits every morning by the elephants... everything you need was catered for. Would definitely go to the same house again.”

Another guest highlighted the lodge's ambiance:

> “The location was excellent. The facilities and ambience of the house was amazing.”

## Prime Location in Mjejane Game Reserve

Situated along the Crocodile River, Jacana River Lodge offers guests front-row seats to the wonders of the African bush. The permeable fences between Mjejane Game Reserve and Kruger National Park allow wildlife to roam freely, enhancing the safari experience. Guests often witness elephants, hippos, and other wildlife from the comfort of the lodge's deck.
