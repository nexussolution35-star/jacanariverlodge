# Experience the Ultimate Safari Retreat at Jacana River Lodge

![](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/bush-braai-e1605708473924.jpg)

Nestled within the breathtaking Mjejane Game Reserve, Jacana River Lodge offers an unparalleled safari experience. Overlooking the majestic Crocodile River, our luxurious 5-bedroom lodge is the perfect destination for families and groups seeking an exclusive self-catering retreat.

## A Sanctuary of Comfort and Luxury

Jacana River Lodge is designed to provide the utmost comfort and luxury. Each of our five en-suite bedrooms is meticulously furnished, ensuring a restful stay. The spacious open-plan living areas include a modern kitchen, a stylish dining room, and a cozy lounge, making you feel right at home. Step out onto our expansive veranda or deck and take in the panoramic views of the African bushveld.

## Unforgettable Wildlife Encounters

Being part of the Mjejane Game Reserve, which lies on the southern boundary of the world-renowned Kruger National Park, Jacana River Lodge offers guests exclusive access to some of the most incredible wildlife experiences. Whether it's a self-drive safari or a guided tour, you’re guaranteed to encounter the Big Five and an array of other fascinating wildlife species.

## Ideal for Family Vacations

Our lodge is designed with families in mind. The kiddies' playroom and jungle gym provide endless entertainment for the little ones, while adults can relax by the pool or enjoy a drink at the upper-level bar. The boma and braai areas are perfect for evening gatherings, where you can share stories of the day’s adventures under the starry African sky.

## Adventure and Relaxation Combined

For those seeking adventure, Jacana River Lodge offers a variety of activities, including game drives, bush walks, and bird watching. If relaxation is what you’re after, our tranquil surroundings provide the perfect backdrop for unwinding and reconnecting with nature.
