# Best Five-Star Self Catering Lodges Near Kruger National Park (Family-Friendly Comparison)

Going on a safari with family or a large group is an unforgettable experience – and choosing the right accommodation can make all the difference. In the Greater Kruger area (including private reserves like Sabi Sands and Mjejane), a handful of five-star, self-catering accommodation near Kruger National Park offer luxury, flexibility, and exclusivity for groups. Unlike traditional all-inclusive lodges, these exclusive-use lodges give you a private space and the freedom to cater for yourselves.

This article compares some of the region’s top-rated options and shows why Jacana River Lodge, located in the Mjejane Game Reserve, stands out for value, comfort, and family-friendly features.

## The Appeal of Five-Star Self-Catering Safari Lodges

Five-star self-catering game lodges in Mpumalanga have surged in popularity for family safaris. Instead of paying per person at a hotel-style lodge, you rent the entire villa or lodge, usually with multiple bedrooms and amenities all to yourselves. This means more privacy and group intimacy – your family or friends have a private pool, deck, boma (firepit), and lounge areas without sharing them with other guests. It’s an ideal setup for multigenerational groups who want a home-away-from-home in the bush.

Another major advantage is value for money. Luxury lodges in reserves like Sabi Sands often charge R20,000+ per person, per night. For example, Sabi Sabi Bush Lodge’s 2025 rate is approximately R26,500 p.p. per night for a suite or R132,000 per night for a 4-sleeper private villa. By contrast, self-catering lodges charge a flat nightly rate for the entire property. With a group of 8–10, the per-person cost drops dramatically – a compelling trade-off for travelers who want luxury without high-end catering bills.

Family-friendliness is another key appeal. While many five-star safari lodges limit or discourage younger children, self-catering lodges are typically designed with families in mind. From spacious layouts and play areas to flexible meal times, these properties are ideal for both parents and kids. There’s no need to worry about formal restaurant settings or structured timetables – it’s a relaxed rhythm where everyone feels at home.

Finally, these lodges are usually located in Big Five territories – either inside or bordering Kruger National Park. Guests can enjoy both guided game drives and access to Kruger’s public self-drive routes. You get all the adventure of a luxury safari with more autonomy over how you spend your days. In short, self-catering lodges offer “the best of both worlds”: a full safari experience with the freedom of a private home.
