Nestled on the banks of the Crocodile River, the [Mjejane Game Reserve](../mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/) is one of South Africa’s hidden gems, offering an unparalleled experience for nature enthusiasts. For families and large groups seeking a private, luxurious retreat, Jacana River Lodge provides an ideal solution. Located within the world-renowned Greater Kruger National Park, the lodge offers both adventure and relaxation with the added privacy of a self-catering five-star villa.

## Why Mjejane Game Reserve is a Top Destination for Families

As one of Mpumalanga’s premier game reserves, Mjejane Game Reserve boasts over 10,000 hectares of protected wildlife habitat. It shares an unfenced border with the Kruger National Park, allowing for free movement of wildlife between the two areas. Visitors have the opportunity to see the Big Five (lion, leopard, rhino, elephant, and buffalo) along with a variety of other animals and birds in their natural habitat.

Here’s why Mjejane Game Reserve is particularly suited for families and large groups:

* **Proximity to Nature:** The lodge offers front-row access to the African wilderness.
* **Self-Drive Safari Routes:** Families can explore the reserve at their own pace.
* **Secure and Private Accommodation:** With Jacana River Lodge's gated property, families can relax in safety.
* **Educational Opportunities for Kids:** Wildlife spotting and eco-tourism experiences are great for learning.

## What Activities Can You Do in Mjejane Game Reserve?

When staying at Jacana River Lodge, you’ll have access to a wide variety of activities, both on the lodge property and within the Mjejane Reserve.

Here are some top options:

* **Game Drives:** Whether self-driven or guided, game drives offer close encounters with elephants, lions, and other iconic species.
* **Birdwatching:** Mjejane is home to over 300 bird species, including fish eagles and hornbills.
* **Bush Walks:** Explore the flora and fauna of the reserve on foot with a professional guide.
* **Sundowner Safaris:** Enjoy a scenic sunset drive, followed by drinks and snacks in the bush.
* **Family-Friendly Stargazing:** With minimal light pollution, Mjejane offers spectacular night skies for stargazing.
