# Jacana River Lodge – A Tour

![Jacana River Lodge game lodge Mpumalanga interior mjejane game reserve (6)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Jacana-River-Lodge-game-lodge-Mpumalanga-interior-mjejane-game-reserve-6.png)![Jacana River Lodge game lodge Mpumalanga interior mjejane game reserve (5)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Jacana-River-Lodge-game-lodge-Mpumalanga-interior-mjejane-game-reserve-5.png)![Jacana River Lodge game lodge Mpumalanga interior mjejane game reserve (4)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Jacana-River-Lodge-game-lodge-Mpumalanga-interior-mjejane-game-reserve-4.png)

When Kruger, the worlds most renowned wildlife park buffers up against your property and the sightings of amazing animals are commonplace from every vantage point, you may feel pressed to provide a suitable answer in terms of building style and décor to marry architecture to its surrounding and yet also highlight those same surroundings and fantastic visual interest.

No easy feat, and not lightly undertaken.

At Jacana River Lodge, which sits on the cusp of the [Mjejane Game Reserve](https://jacanariverlodge.com/mjejane-game-reserve-accommodation-guide/) and overlooks the Kruger National Park beside the Crocodile River, that pressure has culminated in a 5 Star exclusive self-catering lodge experience that infuses the wild bush subtly into spacious living, perfect yet restrained enough for the safari-style lifestyle connoisseurs or boisterous family troupe from the big city.

In feeling with its surroundings in a visual and physical sense,, Jacana River Lodge is also foremost a boon mentally for any weary parent looking to take a well-deserved break and bring along all their offspring for some much need R&R.

Its practical yet sophisticated nature in terms of décor alludes to the state of mind of its frequent guests: ready to enjoy the amazing Mjejane bush and every opportunity that it offers while not being to fussy about everything, or being too precious. Your standard parent then. As family time is all about connecting, with a 5 Star Self Catering lodge, what more would you need? Jacana River Lodge was built by a family and with families in mind, so it functions well to suite the needs of a bustling group of individuals of different ages and different activity requirements.

Its sports a spectacularly well-positioned rim-flow swimming pool that gets the odd ogle from interested elephants passing by.

Although in the middle of one of the most wild-life dense areas on the planet, the Lodge still has ample room to run including a walled courtyard, a lawn and even a jungle gym and kiddies’ playroom for the little ones.

Whether you are dining in the dining room or on the veranda alfresco and letting your eyes flit over the beautiful scene as a warm braai-fire crackles to the bushvelds twilight chorus, Jacana River Lodge does bring you that much closer to the amazing experience of the Mjejane Game Reserve and Kruger National Park, something that few other  [game lodges in Mpumalanga](https://jacanariverlodge.com/) can match so expertly.
