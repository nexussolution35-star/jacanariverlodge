## Why Self-Catering Accommodation Near Kruger National Park Works for Families

Traditional safari lodges can be wonderful, but their format does not suit every group. A large family can have young children, teenagers, parents and grandparents travelling together, all with different routines and appetites.

Self-catering changes the equation.

At Jacana River Lodge, guests have a fully equipped kitchen and the freedom to organise meals themselves. Breakfast can happen when everyone is ready. Lunch can be simple after a morning out, and evenings can be spent cooking together or enjoying a braai.

For parents, that flexibility can be more valuable than having every meal prescribed by an accommodation package. It allows the holiday to develop around the people travelling rather than requiring the family to continually reorganise itself around meal times.

Location remains fundamental to any safari holiday.

Jacana River Lodge is situated within Mjejane Game Reserve on the banks of the Crocodile River. Wildlife, including the Big Five, moves between Mjejane and Kruger National Park, giving guests the opportunity to experience the bush from the reserve itself.

Group accommodation can become complicated surprisingly quickly.

Jacana River Lodge balances those needs with five en-suite bedrooms accommodating a maximum of 10 guests.

This makes the lodge particularly suited to multi-generational families, two households holidaying together or a group of friends who want the safari experience without sacrificing personal space.
