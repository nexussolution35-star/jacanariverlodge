## Mjejane Dam

Mjejane dam is a popular sundowner spot   
on the reserve

There is an abundance of birds around the dam as well as Crocodile, Elephant, Waterbuck and large pods of Hippo that watch you as you sip on your favourite G&T

Mjejane Game Reserve is full of wildlife that are moving in from the Northern parts of the Kruger National Park as the grasslands get scarce to the end of the winter. Look out for the following rear species. Aquatic Turtles, Water Monitor, Africa Fish Eagle, all the Kingfisher species and the famous Jacana
