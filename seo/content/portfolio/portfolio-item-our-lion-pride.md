# Our Lion Pride

Mjejane Private Nature Reserve is home to a large pride of Lions. This pride is made up of 3 large males, 6 females and 16 cubs

We have had the privilege of seeing the Lions on a weekly basis. Here is one of the cubs having a go at a sibling. They are very playful and even try their luck with the big males sometimes
