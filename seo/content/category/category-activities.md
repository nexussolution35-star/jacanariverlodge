# Activities

#### Game Viewing

Wildlife, including the Big 5, roam freely between the KNP and Mjejane Game Reserve. Jacana River Lodge has its own private, exclusive game viewing vehicle to enjoy ± 4 000 hectares of pristine African bush. The area is world-renowned for birding, game spotting and photography enthusiasts!

#### Other Activities

##### Kingsland Recreational Area

Kingsland is a safe, fenced-in area within Mjejane game reserve with a wide variety of fauna and flora indigenous to South Africa.

There are several different facilities on site that can be utilized by visitors. For example the mountain bike trails, hiking and trail running for the active adventurous visitor. Or, the braai and picnic area for families.

##### Guided bush walks in the Kruger National Park

Experience an unspoilt African Walking Safari on foot with Mjejane Game Reserve. The twice-weekly guided bush walks will show you what ‘up close and personal’ really means! Join us every Tuesday and Thursday morning.

A whole new world awaits those who exit their 4×4 and tackle the veld on foot. While a game drive is an exciting experience, nothing beats the quiet intimacy of encountering wildlife eye-to-eye in their own domain.
