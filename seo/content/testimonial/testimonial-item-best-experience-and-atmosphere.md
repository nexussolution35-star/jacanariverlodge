###### BEST EXPERIENCE AND ATMOSPHERE

[![Chadè Hill, visited Jacana River Lodge five star self catering accommodation going on an Africa Safari.](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Chadè-Hill-480x480.jpg)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Chadè-Hill.jpg)

I went to Jacana lodge earlier September, and what an experience. No photos or reviews could truly describe the beauty and the atmosphere of this place.

From the amazing view of the crocodile river where all manor of animals large and small come to drink water from the river, to the luxurious bedrooms and entertainment areas. I never wanted to leave the upstairs patio where I could drink cocktails and watch the sun go down over the river.

During my visit I went on a game drive and it was defiantly the best one I have ever been on. The custom-made game viewer was so comfortable it just made the trip so enjoyable. On our game drive we saw a large number of animals from giraffe to rhino. But my personal favourite was the African Elephant, we saw baby elephants running towards their mother as they realised that she had already walked a distance away from them.
