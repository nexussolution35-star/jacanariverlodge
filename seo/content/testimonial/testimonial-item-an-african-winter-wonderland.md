###### An African Winter Wonderland!!

[![Trunelle Beukes, baby, Mariska Claassen, Jacana Lodge Game Veiwer](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Trunelle-Beukes-Mariska-Claassen-baby-480x480.jpg)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Trunelle-Beukes-Mariska-Claassen-baby.jpg)

Our visit to Jacana River Lodge in the Mjejane Game Reserve was truly one to remember: a ‘Winter Wonderland’ in true African Style with relaxed sunsets overlooking a breath-taking dam, where you are met with a curtain of Egyptian Geese taking flight as you arrive through the true African Bushveld. On return from the Game Drive, you arrive at the lodge, where civilisation meets nature in perfect harmony.
