###### Truly a five star Authentic African Experience

[![Madelien de Wet Testimonial](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Madelien-de-Wet-480x480.jpg)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Madelien-de-Wet.jpg)

How do you start by reviewing the best place in Africa? Photos do not do justice to the beauty and magnificence of this house. All the appliances, bedding and furniture is of the best quality and truly understated elegance. You shall want for nothing at Jacana.

Small touches like the children’s play area and wheel chair access makes this house feel like a home. The location of Jacana River Lodge, makes this a totally private stay, something that is unheard of so close to the Kruger National Park. This is where you can sit on the veranda or in the pool and just watch the world go by – usually with Elephants right on your doorstep. During our stay we saw Elephants, Leopards, Rhino and Lions, almost on a daily basis. We were easily reminded, in this most special setting just what a privilege it is to witness the lives of some of our most precious natural assets. To sit and watch an African sunset on Jacana’s amazing upstairs entertainment deck has left us with such special memories and family moments to treasure. Lying on a pure white Egyptian cotton bed, looking out over the Crocodile River teeming with wildlife, was a fantasy I only thought possible in films.

The amazing setting is matched only by the house itself. Truly a five star Authentic African Experience.
