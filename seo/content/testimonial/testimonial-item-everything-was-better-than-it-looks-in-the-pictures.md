###### Everything was better than it looks in the pictures

[![Jacana River Lodge Reviews](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Simone-480x480.png)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Simone.png)

We spent a long weekend at Jacana River Lodge which was absolutely amazing. The house is extremely well equipped and beautifully furnished. The [view](/gallery/) overlooks the crocodile river and we spent many hours animal watching from the patio. Everything was better than it looks in the pictures and all around a five star experience. Mariska is a fantastic host and promptly communicated at all times. We will definitely be back! I booked through [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.co.za/users/show/166556368)
