###### It surpassed all our expectations

[![](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/WhatsApp-Image-2019-11-14-at-5.01.12-AM-744x480.jpeg)](https://jacanariverlodge.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/WhatsApp-Image-2019-11-14-at-5.01.12-AM-1024x661.jpeg)

Initially we thought the price per night was a bit high however once you get to Jacana one can immediately see and feel your monies worth. The house inviting warm open and airy very spacious without loosing any of its appeal. Everything in the house is high end and the attention to detail is of the highest quality. I would highly recommend it over and over, It surpassed all our expectations… all we can say is WOW amazing on point
