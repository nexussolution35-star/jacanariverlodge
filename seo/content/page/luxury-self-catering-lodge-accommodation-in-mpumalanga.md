#### What makes Jacana’s luxury self-catering lodge in Mpumalanga different?

There are many reasons why we can confidently say that we are not like your average luxury self-catering lodge in Mpumalanga. And it is not just because we are situated in the heart of the beautiful Mjejane Private Nature Reserve.

At most other luxury lodges, self-catered or not, guests are usually greeted by reception staff and have a reception team available at the lodge to help them. At Jacana, while we have staff available, they are not at the lodge itself. We’ve arranged the lodge this way so that our guests get to enjoy the maximum amount of privacy throughout their stay.

The lodge does have cleaning staff available, and should you have any additional requirements, you can contact the lodge team to discuss your specific needs.
