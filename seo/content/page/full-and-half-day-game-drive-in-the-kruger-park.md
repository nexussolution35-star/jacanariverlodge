### Deciding if a half day safari in the Kruger National Park is for you

  

The game drive experience is something unlike other. Travelling slowly through the park, following along the dusty, history laden tracks that criss-cross this ancient nature conservation effort, all the while keeping an eye out for wildlife, is a superb way to spend a morning or an afternoon.

  

If you are on the fence about whether a guided half day safari in the Kruger Park is something you should partake in, perhaps this will help.

• A half day is more than enough time
8 hours in the park can feel a little overwhelming for some and this type of full day trip is also going to cut into your holiday if you are only staying in the area for a few short days. With a 4 hour trip, which is usually how long a half day tour takes, you can see the best of the park while still having enough time to do your own thing.

• A half day tour can present the best sights
Some think that the only way you can see the best of the wildlife that the Kruger has to offer is to spend more than a week in the park. That’s not exactly true, as a half day tour can quite easily result in seeing the Big 5 and more.

• A half day tour is stress free
Instead of having to worry about planning your trip and organising your drive to make sure you get to all the best wildlife viewing places, your tour will be stress free, allowing you to just live in the moment and have fun with it.
