# Wildlife on your doorstep

Natural game species roam freely between Mjejane’s 4000 ha and the Kruger National Park, especially towards the end of winter when grasslands in the north of the park become sparse. Enjoy wonderful game drives in our 10-seater game viewer in Big 5 country!
