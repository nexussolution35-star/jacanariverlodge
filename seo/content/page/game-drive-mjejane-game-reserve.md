### Book a Jacana River Lodge Game Drive Experience in the Mjejane Game Reserve

##### This game drive is exclusively offered to guests staying at Jacana River Lodge.

  

Whether it is a morning spent out on the open plains of the Mjejane Game Reserve, or a sunset game drive along the banks of the Crocodile River, a guided drive is one of those experiences that everyone visiting this corner of the world should book.

The Mjejane Game Reserve is a one of a kind destination. As a part of the Kruger Concession area, the Mjejane is special place. The 4000 hectares that make up the game reserve are renowned for being the best for wildlife viewing, and one of the best ways to take in all the sights and sounds, while breathing in that pure Bushveld air, is by embarking on a half day game drive.

At Jacana River Lodge, we provide our guests with an unforgettable stay. At our luxurious, family friendly accommodation, you can look forward to plenty of regenerating downtime. In the peace and quiet of the Mjejane Private Game Reserve, you can enjoy exclusive wildlife sightings, well away from the crowds that frequent other nearby nature reserves.

  

### Mjejane Game Reserve Safaris

  

Although other parks in the area, such as the Kruger, also offer half day game drives, a Mjejane game reserve safari is a unique experience. Taking place in some of the best wildlife viewing areas, you are sure to see plenty of extraordinary wildlife and leave the game drive having had a fulfilling safari experience.

  

Just a morning or an afternoon out on safari (which is usually the amount of time considered to be a half day game drive), is enough time to see the best of what the reserve has to offer. The Mjejane is home to every animal that you could possibly imagine seeing, including the famous Big 5, and all sorts of unusual critters.

  

The benefit of a guided game drive is that you can sit back and relax. You don’t have to worry about keeping your eyes on the road and you also don’t have to worry about getting lost along the way. The guides who will take you on your Mjejane Game Reserve safari are experienced and they know how to treat guests to a most enjoyable time. With their immense knowledge, you will be in for a treat as you can learn more about what you see on your safari.

**Mjejane Game Reserve safaris** take place in a proper safari vehicle, giving you maximum viewing potential while also allowing you to really take in the atmosphere that the reserve is so well known for. A guided Mjejane Game Reserve safari is a great way to bond with your family and friends. Seated in the comfort of an open safari vehicle, guests will share in the excitement of the animal sighting and the experience will certainly be something worth chatting about for hours afterwards.

Should you wish to include a Mjejane Game Reserve safari in your stay at Jacana River Lodge, let us know while you are making your booking. If you have any other questions about your stay and what you can expect, let us know and we will be more than happy to assist you.
