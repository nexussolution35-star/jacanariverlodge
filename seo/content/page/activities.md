# ACTIVITIES

#### **WILDLIFE**

  

Wildlife, including the Big 5, roam freely between the KNP and Mjejane Game Reserve. Jacana River Lodge has its own private, exclusive game viewing vehicle to enjoy ± 4 000 hectares of pristine African bush. The area is world-renowned for birding, game spotting and photography enthusiasts!
