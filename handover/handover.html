2. Old structure vs new structure

Before

The old site

  • A 9-item menu with dropdowns that mixed topics, plus two PDF files listed as menu items.
  • No silos: pages on the same topic didn’t link to each other.
  • 13 pages had no menu item and could only be found through Google (for example Kruger day drives, Panorama tours, the Irving Knight and Lihlosi packages).
  • 21 pages had no links from any other page.
  • Long text pages with one image, and repeated sections and reviews.
  • A 137-post blog, most posts on one topic (self-catering family stays), competing with the homepage.

After

The new site

  • Three silos, Stay, Activities and Mjejane Reserve, each with a hub page.
  • A mega menu that reaches every page.
  • Linking rules: hub ↔ page and page ↔ sibling within a silo, never across silos. Book Now and Contact can be linked from anywhere.
  • Breadcrumbs on every page, and a “more in this section” photo carousel before the footer.
  • Every page rebuilt from the homepage’s sections, with the live SEO text kept but organised: drop-downs, numbered lists, photo blocks.
  • The same enquiry form at the end of every page.

5. What we changed

Structure & SEO

  • Three silos with a mega menu, breadcrumbs and breadcrumb schema.
  • Every live URL kept, so rankings carry over.
  • 13 new pages for real search demand or real guest questions.
  • All live text kept, organised into sections and drop-downs instead of long walls of text.
  • Lodge schema (Home, Contact), social sharing cards, one main heading per page, sitemaps.
  • Copy checked three times for accuracy (Mjejane vs Kruger, bridge temporarily closed, prices copied exactly).

Pages

  • Every page uses the homepage’s section designs: intro, image-beside-text blocks, numbered lists, drop-downs, photo rows.
  • Clear tables for rates, cancellation, seasons and package comparisons.
  • Contact page: contact cards, a live Google map beside the form, 24-hour emergency numbers.

Design rules applied site-wide

  • Clean heroes: a title and one line, no buttons.
  • Solid buttons only, never crowded under a text link.
  • Every hero photo unique, and no repeated or near-identical photos on a page.
  • High-quality photos swapped in, with no collages or flyers carrying text.
  • Flat cards: no lift or shadow on hover; only the photo zooms.
  • Reviews quoted word for word, and not repeated on every page.
  • Booking cards only for different contacts (spa, walks office, SANParks, emergency). The form covers the lodge.

Phones

  • Nothing squashed or wider than the screen.
  • Long text shows its opening with a “Read more” link.
  • Tables turn into one card per row.
  • Tappable phone numbers and emails, and the WhatsApp button on every page.

6. How to navigate the new site

  1. Menu (top). Hover or tap Stay, Activities or Mjejane Reserve to see every page in that section. Book Now is always on the right.
  2. Breadcrumb (in the hero). Shows where you are, for example Home › Activities › Kruger Tours & Packages › Irving Knight Safaris. Each step is a link back up.
  3. Page sections. Each page opens with an intro and key facts, then the details. Rust-coloured bars are drop-downs; tap one to open it.
  4. Enquiry form (end of every page). Phone, WhatsApp, email and the message form. On Contact, the form sits beside the map.
  5. “More in this section” (before the footer). A photo carousel of related pages in the same section. Use the arrows or swipe.
  6. Footer. Main pages, contact details, awards and the legal pages.
Quick routes: rates → Stay › Rates & Facilities; Kruger safaris compared → Activities › Kruger Tours & Packages; how to get there → Directions; common questions → FAQ.

7. Updating the site

The site is static HTML: every page is folder/index.html at its live URL. Shared parts (menu, footer, breadcrumbs, related-pages carousel, titles, sitemaps) are generated. After editing pages, run these from the project folder in this order:

CommandWhat it does
python3 tools/header.pyBuilds the menu
python3 tools/footer.pyBuilds the footer and river divider
python3 tools/silo.pyBuilds breadcrumbs and the related-pages carousel
python3 tools/seo.pyBuilds titles, descriptions, canonicals and sitemaps
python3 tools/audit.pyPrints an SEO check (report only)

To add a page, add one line to tools/silo_map.py (its name, parent and section) and run the commands above. The full technical notes are in HANDOFF.md and README.md in the project.
