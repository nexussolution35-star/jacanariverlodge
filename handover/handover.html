jacanariverlodge.com · website handover

How the website was structured before,
and how it is structured now

The old website had a menu but no clear structure: pages on the same topic were spread across different menus, 13 pages could not be reached from the menu at all, and most pages were never linked from other pages. The new website groups every page into three sections (Stay, Activities, Mjejane Reserve), each with its own main page, and links the pages so visitors and Google can always find them. Every page address from the old website is kept, so existing Google rankings carry over.

2. The old website: what was wrong

The numbers match the menu on the previous page.

  1. Certificates filed as pages. “Star Grading” and “Tourism Memberships” opened PDF files from the menu. They are trust badges and belong in the footer.
  2. Wildlife under Activities. Wildlife & Sightings is about the reserve, not something you book.
  3. Book Now inside the reserve menu, as well as in the main menu.
  4. Birding under the reserve. Birding is an activity, with its own guides and walks.
  5. “Review Us” in the main menu. A link off the website took the place of a real page.
  6. Whole products hidden. Kruger day drives, Panorama tours, six packages, the bridge page and even the About page had no menu item and no links.
  7. The blog competed with the main pages for the same searches.

How often other pages linked to each page

Links from the content of other pages and posts (menu and footer not counted). Google mostly follows these links.

4. The new website: how pages link

The structure only works if the links follow it. Every page follows the same rules, shown here with the Activities section as an example.

Main page ⇄ page

Each section’s main page links to all its pages, and each page links back up.

Related pages

Pages in the same section link to each other, for example Game Drives ⇄ Bush Walks.

Booking is open

Every page can link to Book Now and Contact Us, and every page ends with the same enquiry form.

No links across sections

An activity page doesn’t link sideways to Rates. Visitors move between sections through the menu or a main page.

6. What else we found on the old website

Beyond the structure, the old website had problems a guest would notice:

  • Inaccurate wording. Some copy placed the lodge “in the Kruger National Park”. It is in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve on the Kruger’s boundary. Kruger access and the bridge into the Kruger (temporarily closed) were also described inconsistently.
  • Prices and details that contradict each other. Package prices, group sizes, walk rates, staff hours and an emergency number differ between pages. We listed them separately for the lodge to confirm.
  • Long walls of text with one photo, and the same review repeated on several pages.
  • Low-quality, collage and flyer-style images (photos with text on them), and the same photos used on many pages.
  • Booking paths that compete. Several different booking buttons and forms per page, in different styles, some leading off the website.
  • Contact details you couldn’t tap. Phone numbers and emails were plain text on several pages.
  • A duplicate Activities page at a second address, splitting visitors between two copies.
  • Hard to use on phones: squashed columns, wide tables and long scrolling.

7. How the new website works for guests

Jacana’s website is large: 46 pages plus the blog. Without a clear structure, visitors get lost and leave before they enquire. The new website is built to keep them oriented, build trust, and make getting in touch effortless.

Easy to find your way

Three clear sections in the menu, each page showing where it sits (Home › Activities › …), and related pages offered at the bottom of every page, so there is always a next step.

Easy to read

Every page follows the same pattern: a short intro and key facts first, then the details in sections and drop-downs. Rates and packages are in clear tables. On phones, long text opens with “Read more”.

Builds trust

Accurate, consistent information, high-quality real photos, guest reviews quoted word for word, the 5-star grading and Booking.com award shown in the footer, and one consistent look across the site.

Frictionless contact

The same short enquiry form ends every page. Phone numbers and emails are tappable, a WhatsApp button is always on screen, and Book Now is always in the menu. One clear action per section.
