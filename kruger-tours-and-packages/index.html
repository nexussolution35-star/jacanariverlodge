Kruger & the Panorama Route

Kruger Tours and Safari Packages from Jacana River Lodge

Day trips into the Kruger National Park and along the Panorama Route from the Mjejane Private Game Reserve, on the Kruger’s southern boundary.

Jacana River Lodge is in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve on the Kruger National Park’s southern boundary, between the Malelane and Crocodile Bridge gates. That makes the lodge an easy base for Kruger tours: a guided full- or half-day game drive in the park, a private Kruger drive with Ranger Special Khoza, a full-day safari with Irving Knight Safaris, or a day out on the Panorama Route.

The Mjejane bridge into the Kruger is temporarily closed; the southern Kruger is reached via the Malelane or Crocodile Bridge gates.

The Panorama Route

For a day away from the bush, the Panorama Route takes you to the Blyde River Canyon, the third largest canyon in the world, as well as God’s Window, the Three Rondavels and Bourke’s Luck Potholes. The lodge’s Panorama tours run as a full or half day, and the Kruger & Panorama packages include a full-day tour from about 06:30 to 17:00 with a light lunch. See our guide to the Graskop Gorge Lift and other day trips near Mjejane.
