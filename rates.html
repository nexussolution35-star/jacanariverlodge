Rates & Facilities

Rates & Facilities

Jacana River Lodge is booked on an exclusive-use, self-catering basis — the whole lodge, just for your group, by the weekend or the week.

How It Works

Exclusive-Use, Self-Catering Luxury

Because Jacana is hired exclusively for your party, our rates are quoted per stay rather than per person — and they vary with the season and the number of nights. Tell us your dates and group size and we’ll send a tailored quote, usually the same day.

Rates are for the whole lodge (up to 10 guests) on a self-catering basis. Game drives, bush walks, the mobile bush spa and Kruger safaris are arranged separately and added to your stay on request.
