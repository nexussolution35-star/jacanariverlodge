Rates & Facilities

Rates & Facilities

Jacana River Lodge is booked on an exclusive-use, self-catering basis — the whole lodge, just for your group, by the weekend or the week.

How It Works

Exclusive-Use, Self-Catering Luxury

Because Jacana is hired exclusively for your party, our rates are quoted per stay rather than per person — and they vary with the season and the number of nights. Tell us your dates and group size and we’ll send a tailored quote, usually the same day.

Rates are for the whole lodge (up to 10 guests) on a self-catering basis. Game drives, bush walks, the mobile bush spa and Kruger safaris are arranged separately and added to your stay on request.

Featured Package

Jacana River Lodge 5-Night Package

An exclusive 5-night escape in the heart of the Mjejane Private Game Reserve — 5-star self-catering luxury, two game drives, and your choice of adventures.

Unparalleled Accommodation

Are you ready to immerse yourself in the beauty of the African wilderness? Jacana River Lodge presents an exclusive 5-night package that promises an unforgettable adventure in the heart of the Mjejane Private Game Reserve. Indulge in 5-star luxury as you spend five nights in our breathtaking self-catering lodge. With five beautifully appointed bedrooms, each boasting en-suite facilities and top-notch amenities, your comfort is our priority. Wake up to the soothing sounds of nature, surrounded by the sights and scents of the wild.

Thrilling Wildlife Safaris

Embark on two exhilarating game drives within the Mjejane Private Game Reserve, led by our experienced guides. Get up close and personal with Africa’s iconic wildlife and witness the untamed beauty of the animal kingdom.

Choose Your Adventure

But that’s not all — your African experience doesn’t end with the game drives. We offer an array of exciting additional activities for you to choose from. Each adventure comes at an extra cost, allowing you to tailor your stay and make your journey even more memorable. Select from guided bush walks, the mobile bush spa, full-day Kruger safaris, birding, the Panorama Route and more to enhance your safari.
