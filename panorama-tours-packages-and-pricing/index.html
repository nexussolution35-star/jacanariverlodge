Mpumalanga’s Panorama Route

Panorama Tours

Welcome to the breathtaking Panorama Route, an iconic destination in the heart of Mpumalanga province, South Africa. This enchanting road winds through awe-inspiring landscapes, cultural gems and unforgettable natural wonders. Prepare for a journey where every turn reveals breathtaking vistas and hidden treasures.

But the Panorama Route is more than just the Blyde River Canyon. It’s a treasure trove of diverse attractions. Marvel at the cascading splendour of Lisbon Falls and Berlin Falls, where the rushing waters create a symphony of sound and movement. Lose yourself in the tranquillity of Bourke’s Luck Potholes, a geological wonderland of swirling rock formations, potholes and waterfalls.

More about Panorama Tours

At the heart of the Panorama Route lies the magnificent Blyde River Canyon, the third largest canyon in the world. Carved over centuries by the mighty Blyde River, this natural marvel is a testament to the raw power and beauty of Mother Nature. As you stand at the canyon’s edge, gazing out over its majestic cliffs and lush greenery, you’ll be captivated by its sheer grandeur.

Delve into South Africa’s rich cultural heritage in the historic mining town of Pilgrim’s Rest, frozen in time and offering a glimpse into the gold rush era of the late 1800s, and visit the Echo Caves, a hidden gem where ancient stalactites and stalagmites create a mesmerising underground world.

Panorama Tours Pricing and Packages

Answers to some frequently asked questions about the Panorama Route: entrance fees, recommended duration, the cost of specific attractions and more.

1. How much is the entrance fee for Panorama Route?

The entrance fee for the Panorama Route is typically not applicable. It is a scenic road that traverses stunning landscapes, viewpoints and attractions, allowing visitors to enjoy the beauty without an entrance fee.

2. How much does it cost to enter the Three Rondavels?

There is no specific entrance fee to enter the Three Rondavels. These iconic rock formations can be admired from designated viewpoints along the Panorama Route without incurring additional charges.

3. How much is God’s Window entrance fee?

The entrance fee for God’s Window may vary. It is advisable to check with the local authorities, visitor information centres or the official website for the most accurate and up-to-date information regarding entrance fees.

4. How many days do you need for Panorama Route?

To fully experience the beauty and attractions along the Panorama Route, it is recommended to allocate at least two to three days. This allows for a leisurely exploration, including visits to the major viewpoints, waterfalls and other attractions.

5. How much does it cost to go to Blyde River Canyon?

The cost to visit Blyde River Canyon can vary depending on the specific activities or tours chosen. Pricing can differ for guided tours, boat trips or self-guided exploration. It is advisable to check with local tour operators or visitor centres for accurate and up-to-date pricing information.

6. How much does it cost to enter Mac Mac Falls?

Visiting Mac Mac Falls usually does not require an entrance fee. Visitors can enjoy the natural beauty and splendour of the falls without incurring any additional costs.

7. Who are the Three Rondavels from Graskop?

The Three Rondavels are a set of distinctive rock formations located near Graskop. They are named after their resemblance to the traditional round African houses called “rondavels”. These geological wonders offer stunning views of the Blyde River Canyon and surrounding landscapes.

8. What time of day is best to visit God’s Window?

To experience God’s Window at its best, it is recommended to visit early in the morning or late in the afternoon. At these times the lighting conditions are often ideal, and you can enjoy breathtaking panoramic views with fewer crowds.

9. How much is Graskop bungee jumping?

The pricing for Graskop bungee jumping experiences can vary depending on the specific operator or adventure company. For the most accurate and up-to-date pricing, it is advisable to contact local bungee jumping operators or tour providers.

10. How much is the entrance fee for Bourkes potholes?

The entrance fee for Bourke’s Potholes can vary, and it is recommended to check with the relevant authorities or visitor information centres for the most accurate and up-to-date information regarding entrance fees.

11. How much is the entrance fee to Bourkes Luck Potholes?

As with Bourke’s Potholes, the entrance fee to Bourke’s Luck Potholes may vary. For the current entrance fee, it is advisable to consult the relevant authorities or visit the official website for accurate pricing details.

12. Where are the Three Rondavels located?

The Three Rondavels are located near the town of Graskop along the Panorama Route in Mpumalanga, South Africa.
