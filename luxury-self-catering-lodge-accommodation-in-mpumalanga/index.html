Paradise on the ‘Croc’

A five-star self-catering lodge in Mpumalanga that is yours alone: five king en-suite rooms, an infinity pool and wide views over the Crocodile River.

Lazy days at Jacana start with early mornings, the sun shining into the bedrooms and the sound of wildlife and indigenous birds starting their morning routines. The Crocodile River moves slowly past the lodge while you get ready for the day’s first game drive, and sets the pace for the most peaceful holiday you will ever have.

Deep in the heart of the South African bushveld lies Jacana River Lodge, an exclusive self-catered game lodge in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve. We are the perfect escape from the world: the ideal getaway for those who love the bush and who are looking for a place to spend a few days enjoying the wonders that nature has to offer.

In a secluded spot right on the banks of the Crocodile River, Jacana River Lodge is a peaceful place with a welcoming atmosphere and luxurious lodgings. Completely self-catered, with all the cutlery, utensils and cooking space needed to create every meal and do the washing up afterwards, it lets guests indulge in a real escape from the rush and stress of city living.

With the lodge overlooking the river, it is not at all uncommon to see all sorts of wildlife arriving for an early-morning or late-afternoon drink. Throughout the day you can enjoy private wildlife viewing right from the swimming pool or deck, and when the mood strikes, a game drive can be arranged.

Our lodge is also child-friendly, making it the ideal family holiday destination. On-site amenities include all the essentials: a spacious infinity swimming pool looking out over the game reserve, a large braai area, secure parking and free Wi-Fi. Jacana River Lodge is a 5-star self-catering establishment, and there is no better place for a relaxing family holiday or just a lekker kuier.

Why Guests Choose Jacana

There are many reasons why we can confidently say that we are not like your average luxury self-catering lodge in Mpumalanga.

More about staying at Jacana

Self-catered luxury Mpumalanga accommodation is one of the most sought-after types of accommodation in the province. It is ideal for family getaways and for trips with large groups of friends, who get greater control over how they plan their trip and plenty of space to relax in. Game drives in a private nature reserve can be extra special too: there are no crowds to fight through, and most private parks are home to larger wildlife populations, which makes spotting animals a lot easier.

At most other luxury lodges, self-catered or not, guests are greeted by reception staff and have a reception team at the lodge. At Jacana, while we have staff available, they are not at the lodge itself. We’ve arranged the lodge this way so that our guests enjoy the maximum amount of privacy throughout their stay. The lodge does have cleaning staff, and if you have any additional requirements, you can contact the lodge team to discuss your needs.

To get the most out of your time here, book more than a 2-night stay. The lodge easily becomes a home away from home, and it is perfect for the more independent adventurer.

Our home in the reserve

Life in the Mjejane

The Mjejane Private Nature Reserve is a slice of heaven. Situated on the boundaries of the legendary Kruger National Park, it forms part of the Greater Kruger, a concession area made up of various pieces of land which have, over the years, become part of the Kruger conservation effort. This area is especially beautiful because a part of the Crocodile River runs through it.

About our home in the Mjejane

The location makes it a game-viewing paradise. Within the thick riverine forest, bird and animal life flourish. Green pigeons flutter amongst the leafy trees, and purple-crested louries call from the canopy. Leopards and elephants frequent the riverbanks in search of water, the grunting of hippo pods carries up to the lodge, and the river draws antelope, wildebeest and buffalo, with predators never far behind.

We chose to make our home here because we have a deep connection to this beautiful place. Our lodge has been built to accommodate only a select number of guests at a time, ensuring utter peace and privacy, away from the tourist crowds. This exclusivity means wildlife sightings are experiences unique to those who see them.

A word about the Mjejane

Africans hold a special place in their hearts for the wilderness and the wildlife that characterise our home. This wide stretch of land was set aside by Chief Lugedlane with the intention of preserving the land and its wildlife for future generations to enjoy: an inheritance in a way that is so distinctly African.

The land still belongs to the Mjejane community, and those who have built their self-catering game lodges here understand the importance of honouring the commitment to preserving it.

At our peaceful lodge, guests stay close to nature, so close that they feel part of the natural cycle. Wake to the sounds of elephants near your bedroom window and fall asleep to the thrilling roar of lions, the cackle of hyenas and the haunting calls of owls.
