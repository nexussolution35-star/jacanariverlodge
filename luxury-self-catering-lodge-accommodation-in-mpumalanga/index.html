About Jacana River Lodge

An exclusive self-catered game lodge in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve — your home in the heart of the South African bushveld.

Welcome to Jacana

Welcome to our self-catering game lodge in Mpumalanga. Welcome Home

Deep in the heart of the South African bushveld lies Jacana River Lodge, an exclusive self-catered game lodge situated in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve. We are the perfect escape from the world — the ideal getaway for those who love the bush and who are looking for a place to spend a few days enjoying the wonders that nature has to offer.

Welcome Home

Life in the bushveld is never hurried, and at our self-catering game lodge in Mpumalanga, guests can look forward to exquisite accommodation and exciting game viewing. Our lodge has 5 en-suite bedrooms, which include 1 wheelchair-friendly room, and open-plan living spaces with a sprawling kitchen, comfortable lounge and a dining room big enough to fit the whole family.

Our lodge is also child-friendly, making us the ideal family holiday destination. On-site amenities include all the essentials — a spacious infinity swimming pool which looks out over the game reserve, a large braai area, secure parking and free Wi-Fi access. Jacana River Lodge is a 5-star self-catering establishment, and there is no better place to enjoy a relaxing family holiday or just a lekker kuier.

About our home in the Mjejane

The Mjejane Private Nature Reserve is a slice of heaven. Situated on the boundaries of the legendary Kruger National Park, it forms part of the Greater Kruger — a concession area made up of various pieces of land which have, over the years, become part of the Kruger conservation effort. This area is especially beautiful because a part of the Crocodile River runs through it.

The location makes it a game-viewing paradise. Within the thick riverine forest, bird and animal life flourish — green pigeons flutter amongst the leafy trees, and purple-crested louries call from the canopy. Leopards and elephants frequent the riverbanks in search of water, the grunting of hippo pods carries up to the lodge, and the river draws antelope, wildebeest and buffalo — with predators never far behind.

We chose to make our home here because we have a deep connection to this beautiful place. Our lodge has been built to accommodate only a select number of guests at a time, ensuring utter peace and privacy, away from the tourist crowds. This exclusivity means wildlife sightings are experiences unique to those who see them.

A word about the Mjejane

Africans hold a special place in their hearts for the wilderness and the wildlife that characterise our home. This wide stretch of land was set aside by Chief Lugedlane with the intention of preserving the land and its wildlife for future generations to enjoy — an inheritance in a way that is so distinctly African.

The land still belongs to the Mjejane community, and those who have built their self-catering game lodges here understand the importance of honouring the commitment to preserving it.

Accommodation at Jacana

At our peaceful lodge, guests stay close to nature — so close that they feel part of the natural cycle. Wake to the sounds of elephants near your bedroom window and fall asleep to the thrilling roar of lions, the cackle of hyenas and the haunting calls of owls.

Our self-catering accommodation is the perfect place for a family getaway, and with our full-time domestic worker, Esther, available to help clean up, you can sit back and fully enjoy a well-deserved break. Jacana River Lodge accommodates a maximum of 10 guests at a time, and you can book your stay for a full week or just a weekend — you’ll never have to share your space with strangers.

Why Jacana

What Makes Jacana One of the Best Game Lodges in Mpumalanga?

There are many reasons why Jacana is one lodge that should make your list.

1. Jacana is an affordable place to stay

Considering the size of the lodge and its ability to sleep a big family — and that it is one of the higher-end accommodation options — when you think about Jacana River Lodge as your preferred place to stay, you can budget for it.

2. The perfect lodge for a lengthy safari

When you book our lodge, especially if you are sharing the expenses, you can afford to extend your time in the reserve. The more time you spend here, the more chance you have of seeing all sorts of wildlife you might otherwise have missed.

3. Jacana has personality

From the exquisite views of the Crocodile River to open-plan living areas that embody the full African aesthetic, guests can’t help but feel they have left city life behind for something completely otherworldly.

4. The best game viewing

Jacana is home to the Big 5 and so much more. Whether you head out on a self-drive safari or simply watch the wildlife go by from the deck, you are certain to have all sorts of animals cross your path.

5. Exclusivity

If you are looking to escape the crowds and have a completely private stay, Jacana is the place to be. You and your family will be the only guests here throughout your stay.
