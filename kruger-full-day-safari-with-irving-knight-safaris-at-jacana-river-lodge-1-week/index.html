Package Highlights

Luxurious accommodation: enjoy 4 nights of unparalleled comfort and relaxation at Jacana River Lodge, a 5-star self-catering retreat nestled amid the natural beauty of the Mjejane Game Reserve. Immerse yourself in opulence and tranquillity.

Thrilling game drives: embark on 2 exhilarating game drives within the Mjejane Private Game Reserve, guided by expert rangers who will unlock the secrets of the African bush and provide you with remarkable wildlife sightings, including the iconic Big Five.

Full-day safari with Irving Knight Safaris: prepare for an extraordinary adventure as you journey into the Kruger National Park with Irving Knight Safaris, renowned for their expertise and commitment to providing exceptional wildlife experiences.

  • Private game reserve

Full-Day Game Drive with Irving Knight Safaris

Departure: set out early in the morning to maximise your time in the park. The full-day game drive departs from Jacana River Lodge and takes you deep into the heart of the Kruger National Park, ensuring ample opportunity for wildlife encounters.

Wildlife spectacles: witness the incredible biodiversity of the park as you come face to face with majestic elephants, elusive leopards, mighty lions and a myriad of other fascinating animals. Irving Knight Safaris’ knowledgeable guides will provide insightful commentary, enhancing your safari experience.

Conservation fees: please note that conservation fees are not included in the package price during the low season. These fees contribute to the ongoing preservation and protection of the park’s ecosystem.

Five-Star Self-Catering Accommodation in Mpumalanga

At Jacana River Lodge we take pride in providing a 5-star self-catering experience that exceeds your expectations. Our accommodation offers a 5-bedroom lodge perfect for families. Enjoy the convenience of a fully equipped kitchen, allowing you to prepare delicious meals using fresh local produce.

Nearby Attractions

While staying at Jacana River Lodge you’ll have easy access to the many wonders of Mpumalanga. Explore the world-famous Kruger National Park, where you can witness the incredible diversity of wildlife in its natural habitat, or take a scenic drive along the iconic Panorama Route, with its panoramic views of Blyde River Canyon, God’s Window and the Three Rondavels.

Optional Add-ons

Enhance your self-catering getaway with our optional add-on services and experiences. Embark on a guided safari tour, led by experienced rangers who will unveil the secrets of the African bush, or indulge in a rejuvenating treatment with the Mjejane Mobile Bush Spa and let your cares melt away in a serene setting. Our dedicated staff are here to tailor your stay to your preferences.
