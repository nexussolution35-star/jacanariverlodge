Self-Catering Accommodation near Malelane

An exclusive-use, five-star self-catering lodge for up to 10 guests in the Mjejane Game Reserve on the Kruger’s southern boundary.

Near Malelane, on the N4

How Far Is Jacana from Malelane?

About 38 minutes along the N4

Jacana River Lodge is about 38 minutes’ drive from Malelane, in the Mjejane Game Reserve on the N4 between Malelane and Komatipoort.

We are not in Malelane itself: the lodge sits on the banks of the Crocodile River inside a private game reserve near Hectorspruit, so you stay in the bush and drive into town when you need to.

Into the Kruger

Malelane Gate and the Kruger

The Mjejane Game Reserve sits on the Kruger National Park’s southern boundary, between the Malelane Gate and the Crocodile Bridge Gate, so either gate makes an easy start to a day in the park. Prefer someone else to do the driving? Our activities include full-day Kruger safaris and private Kruger drives with local guides.

You don’t have to leave the reserve to see the Big 5, though. Private game drives in the Mjejane run in the lodge’s own game viewer for R2000 for a group of up to 10 people, from 07:00 or 16:00, booked 3 days in advance. See private game drives.

In town

Shops, Fuel & Golf in Malelane

Jacana is self-catering, so most guests stock up on the way in. Malelane and Komatipoort both have supermarkets, and the towns along the N4 have fuel and supplies.

Golfers can play the Malelane Golf Club: a 9-hole course with beautifully maintained greens, surrounded by indigenous trees and only a club swing away from the Kruger National Park.

Big 5, fence-free

Why Stay in the Mjejane

The Mjejane Game Reserve is home to all of the Big 5. Its 4000 hectares straddle 11 km of the Crocodile River, and all fences between the reserve and the Kruger National Park have been removed, so wildlife moves freely between the two. You get the Kruger’s wildlife without the busy safari crowds.

At Jacana River Lodge the whole lodge is yours: five en-suite bedrooms with king-size beds for up to 10 guests, a fully equipped kitchen, two covered braai areas and a boma, and an infinity pool above the river where elephants and hippos come down to drink. 2026 rates start at R11 950 per night for the whole lodge; see rates and facilities for all the details.
