Top Private Concessions in Kruger National Park for 2024

Published 14 December 2023 · Mjejane Game Reserve Expert

1. Private Concessions Unveiled

Embark on an extraordinary safari adventure as Kruger National Park's private concessions unveil a world of exclusivity and wildlife wonders. Mjejane Game Reserve, spanning 4000 hectares, is a standout destination, offering a unique blend of freedom and flexibility for families and friends.

Flexibility and Freedom:

In Mjejane, the untamed beauty of the African bush unfolds as families and friends enjoy dedicated vehicles and guides, setting their own pace in the vast landscapes teeming with rich biodiversity. The freedom to tailor the safari experience enhances the adventure, making it a perfect escape.

Exclusive Night Safaris and Bushwalks:

As the sun sets over Mjejane, the magic of the bush comes alive during exclusive night safaris. Explore the nocturnal wonders and indulge in guided bushwalks led by experienced rangers, providing an intimate connection with nature, perfect for families seeking adventure.

Uncrowded Luxury:

Mjejane distinguishes itself with limited vehicles, ensuring an uncrowded and exclusive safari experience. Families and large groups can relish the tranquility of the bush, immersed in the sights and sounds of nature without the distraction of crowded safari vehicles.

2. The Self-Catering 5-Star Experience at Jacana River Lodge

Nestled in the heart of Mjejane, Jacana River Lodge offers a self-catering 5-star experience, redefining luxury in the midst of the African bush.

Nature's Spectacle at Mjejane Dam:

Jacana River Lodge provides breathtaking views of Mjejane dam, a popular sundowner spot. The dam is surrounded by an abundance of birds, including Crocodile, Elephant, Waterbuck, and large pods of Hippo, creating a mesmerizing backdrop for enjoying a refreshing G&T.

Magnificent Wildlife Encounters:

Mjejane Game Reserve is a haven for wildlife, with species migrating from the Northern parts of Kruger National Park as winter progresses. Look out for rare species such as Aquatic Turtles, Water Monitor, Africa Fish Eagle, various Kingfisher species, and the iconic Jacana.

Meet the Lion Pride:

Mjejane Game Reserve proudly hosts a large pride of Lions—3 large males, 6 females, and 16 playful cubs. Witness their playful antics, including cubs challenging each other and occasionally trying their luck with the big males.

Verreaux Eagle Owls:

Encounter the majestic Verreaux Eagle Owls, residents of Mjejane Nature Reserve, and witness the growth of their nearly full-grown chicks. Learn about their distinctive features, such as ear tufts for enhanced hearing, and appreciate the family dynamics as the chicks venture out in search of food.

Guide to Mjejane Birding:

Explore the sensational avian wildlife of Mjejane Game Reserve through the expert insights of safari guides. With over 500 bird species, including special treasures unique to Mjejane, birders can delight in the diverse birdlife within the Kruger National Park.

3. Family-Friendly Safari Activities

Mjejane Game Reserve ensures a family-friendly approach to safari activities, catering to diverse interests and age groups.

Flexibility for All Ages:

Specially crafted game drives cater to the diverse interests of all family members. Younger children can engage in interactive learning experiences, while teenagers and adults can delve into more in-depth wildlife encounters.

Bushwalks for the Adventurous:

For families with a taste for adventure, guided bushwalks are a must. Led by experienced rangers, these walks provide a different perspective on the African bush. It's an opportunity to learn about tracking, plant life, and the smaller inhabitants of the reserve.

Educational Programs for Young Explorers:

Mjejane Game Reserve goes the extra mile to make safaris educational and entertaining for younger visitors. Engaging programs introduce them to the wonders of nature, fostering a lifelong appreciation for wildlife conservation.

4. Exploring Kruger's Private Concessions

While Mjejane takes center stage, Kruger National Park boasts several private concessions, each with its unique charm.

Diverse Ecosystems and Wildlife:

These private concessions contribute to the overall richness of Kruger's biodiversity. From the iconic Big 5 to the smaller, often overlooked species, each concession has its own story to tell. Exploring multiple concessions allows visitors to witness the full spectrum of the African wilderness.

Book Your Accommodation:

Make your journey extraordinary by booking your accommodation at Jacana River Lodge. Revel in the self-catering 5-star experience within Mjejane Game Reserve, and create lasting memories in the heart of the African bush. Families and large groups are encouraged to secure their spot for an unforgettable safari adventure.

Facilities at Jacana River Lodge: Your Gateway to Luxury and Comfort

Per Game Drive Facilities:

For an approximate 3-hour game drive in Mjejane Game Reserve, accommodating up to 10 people, book in advance at R1,800 per group. Strict rules ensure respect for animals, visitors, and property.

Veranda, Deck, and Pool:

Step onto the veranda for alfresco dining, relax in a separate seating area with spectacular river views, and enjoy the blissful rim-flow swimming pool.

Boma and Braais:

Choose between covered braai areas or the boma for barbecues. All cooking tools are provided, and firewood is supplied, creating an ideal setting for unforgettable African sunsets.

Service Areas:

Jacana River Lodge goes beyond expectations with additional facilities like a scullery, washing machine, dishwashing machine, and extra fridges for guest use.

Safe Parking:

Undercover parking for three vehicles and a conveniently situated carport make unpacking and accessing the game viewer hassle-free.

Courtyard, Lawn, and Jungle Gym:

Secure your little ones in the courtyard, where they can run around safely on the lawn or enjoy the swings and slide on the jungle gym.

Kiddies Playroom and Small Gym:

An entertainment room keeps the young ones busy with TV, a drawing board, books, games, and a pool table. A small gym with exercise equipment caters to the more active youngsters.

5 En-Suite Bedrooms:

Luxury and comfort await in the lodge's five en-suite bedrooms, featuring king-size beds, bar fridges, coffee stations, air-conditioning, ceiling fans, and mosquito nets. One bedroom is wheelchair-friendly, dedicated to guests with special needs.

Open Plan Living Areas:

Spacious yet homely, the lounge, dining area, and kitchen are fully equipped, ensuring a cozy atmosphere. The kitchen includes a double door fridge/freezer, gas stove, conventional oven, microwave oven, and a Nespresso machine. The lodge is fully serviced, offering cable network and WiFi.

In the heart of Mjejane Game Reserve, Jacana River Lodge stands as a gateway to unforgettable safari adventures. From exclusive wildlife encounters and breathtaking sunsets to the luxurious comforts of the lodge, every moment is crafted for families and large groups seeking the magic of the African bush. Book your extraordinary journey now, and let Mjejane unfold its enchanting stories for you.
