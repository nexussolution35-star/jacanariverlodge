Welcome Home

Welcome to our self-catering game lodge in Mpumalanga. Welcome Home

Deep in the heart of the South African bushveld lies Jacana River Lodge, an exclusive self-catered game lodge in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve — the perfect escape from the world for those who love the bush.

Our 5-bedroom lodge accommodates 10 guests across luxurious en-suite rooms, with a sprawling kitchen, dining room, lounge, veranda, infinity pool, boma and an upper-level bar for those awesome sundowners — plus a one-of-a-kind kiddies playroom and jungle gym. But let’s get to the serious part… the ‘KUIER’.
