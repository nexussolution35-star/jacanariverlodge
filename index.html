Welcome to Jacana River Lodge

A Safari Lodge on the Kruger’s Southern Edge

Paradise on the ‘Croc’

Jacana River Lodge is a private, exclusive-use safari lodge in Mpumalanga, in the Mjejane Game Reserve on the Kruger National Park’s southern boundary, sleeping 10 guests on the banks of the Crocodile River.

Deep in the South African bushveld, along the southern boundary of the Kruger National Park, this self-catering game lodge has five luxurious en-suite bedrooms, and it is never shared: when you book, the whole lodge is yours. Graded five stars, it is the perfect escape for families and friends who love the bush.

Mjejane forms part of the Greater Kruger, where all of the Big 5 roam. You may well spot them from the comfort of the veranda.

Rates & Packages

Life in the bushveld is never hurried. Cook together in the sprawling kitchen, gather in the lounge and dining room, cool off in the infinity pool above the river, then head up to the upper-level bar for sundowners before the fire is lit in the boma.

Little ones have a one-of-a-kind playroom and jungle gym. And then there’s the serious part: the ‘kuier’. There is no real English translation for this Afrikaans word, but it’s best described as having a great time with friends and family.

Private game drives, bush walks and guided day trips into the Kruger can all be arranged during your stay.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to plan your stay at Jacana River Lodge.

How many guests can stay at Jacana River Lodge?

The lodge sleeps up to 10 guests in five en-suite bedrooms with king-size beds. It is booked on an exclusive-use basis, so the whole lodge is yours and is never shared with other guests.

Is the lodge self-catering?

Yes. The kitchen is fully equipped with a double-door fridge/freezer, gas stove, conventional oven, microwave and a Nespresso machine. There are two covered braai areas and a boma, with cooking tools and firewood supplied.

What makes Jacana one of the best game lodges in Mpumalanga?

Jacana River Lodge sits on the banks of the Crocodile River. It is secluded, big enough for the whole family and more than capable of meeting your luxury expectations, even though it is self-catered.

It is affordable for a big group sharing the cost, so you can extend your time in the reserve. It has real personality, with exquisite river views and open-plan living areas. The Mjejane is home to the Big 5 and so much more, and even from the deck you are likely to see all sorts of animals pass by. And it is exclusive: you and your family are the only guests at the lodge for your whole stay.

What wildlife can we see in the Mjejane?

The Mjejane Private Game Reserve is on the southern boundary of the Kruger National Park, so guests can spot just about every animal the area has to offer, including all of the Big 5. Because the reserve is so close to the Crocodile River, wildlife sightings are especially bountiful, and you may even see the Big 5 from the comfort of the lodge.

Why stay in the Mjejane instead of inside the Kruger?

With its more exclusive appeal, the Mjejane is home to some of the most luxurious and private self-catering game lodges in Mpumalanga. Guests looking for a quiet escape, away from the busier parts of the Kruger, wake up to the sounds of elephants near their windows and fall asleep to the roar of lions, the cackling of hyenas and the calls of owls.

What are the rates for 2026?

Rates are for the whole lodge per night:

  • Low season, Monday – Thursday: R11 950 per night for the lodge
  • Low season, Friday – Sunday: R14 550 per night for the lodge
  • Peak season (long weekends & school holidays), Monday – Sunday: R14 550 per night for the lodge

Rates are subject to change. See Rates & Packages for current specials.

How do deposits and payment work?

A 50% reservation deposit is required to confirm your booking, payable within 24 hours. The remaining 50% balance is strictly payable 60 days prior to arrival. A fully refundable breakage deposit of R5 000.00 also applies, refunded after check-out subject to damage or breakage.

Are there any reserve fees on top of the rate?

Yes. Mjejane Game Reserve charges a conservation fee of R350 per vehicle (seven-day cycle) and R60 per guest per night, payable on arrival at the main gate. No cash is accepted at the gate; card payments only.

Can we book game drives?

Yes. Private game drives in the Mjejane Game Reserve cost R2 000 for a group of up to 10 people and run for about three hours, either from 07:00 or from 16:00. Drives must be booked three days in advance. Guided bush walks, Kruger day safaris and a mobile bush spa can also be arranged; see all activities.

Is the lodge suitable for children and guests with special needs?

Yes. The lodge is child-friendly, with a one-of-a-kind kiddies' playroom and a jungle gym. It is also wheelchair-friendly, with one bedroom dedicated to guests with special needs.

Is there Wi-Fi and air-conditioning?

The lodge is fully serviced with a cable network and Wi-Fi. Every bedroom has air-conditioning, a ceiling fan, a bar fridge, a coffee station and mosquito nets.

Where is the lodge?

Jacana River Lodge is in the Mjejane Private Game Reserve near Hectorspruit, Mpumalanga, on the banks of the Crocodile River along the southern boundary of the Kruger National Park. It is about 4 hours from Johannesburg and 1 hour from Kruger Mpumalanga International Airport, between the Malelane and Crocodile Bridge gates. See our directions for the route.
